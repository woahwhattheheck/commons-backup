## BID / PARTNER-FIRST / NO-BID rule

### Direct BID only if all are true

- the official current RFP and Amendment #1 are recovered and source-bound;
- the bidder can truthfully satisfy legal/entity/licensing/tax/debarment requirements;
- mandatory references and commercial-history thresholds are met;

- the packet permits the AI/software/services categories actually being offered;
- required security/privacy/accessibility obligations can be evidenced;
- support, warranty, marketing, sales reporting and administrative-fee obligations are operationally supportable;
- pricing has an owner-approved cost/revenue model and does not assume the reported cooperative usage becomes actual sales;
- an owner separately authorizes every external representation and submission.

### PARTNER-FIRST if

The technical AI/software fit is strong but the prime bidder lacks required public-sector references, commercial catalog maturity, reseller status, insurance scale, nationwide support or contract-marketing capacity. In that case, qualify an established prime or channel partner and position Commons/Titan work as an AI/agent/integration delivery component only after the solicitation's teaming/subcontracting rules are confirmed.

### NO-BID if

A mandatory requirement cannot be truthfully satisfied by closing, including entity eligibility, minimum business history, references, certifications, insurance, commercial-product status, nationwide delivery/support or a prohibited teaming structure.
