**State:** HOLD — credible teaming road identified; partner participation and Appendix C references are not secured.

**Buyer:** South Central Workforce Development Board (SCWDB), Kentucky  
**Procurement:** Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Network — AI Industry Accelerator and AI-Powered Career Readiness Training Services  
**Primary source:** https://southcentralworkforce.com/request-for-proposals  
**Published Q&A:** https://assets.zyrosite.com/mjEQjVPDyGsZw4jM/kentucky-ai-workforce-readiness_rfp-qs-as_2026.08.29_final-nNf7aqGWtJgRqD9J.pdf  
**Proposal deadline:** 2026-09-18 16:00 Central  
**Expected evaluation:** 2026-09-21 through 2026-10-02  
**Anticipated term:** written notice to proceed through 2029-06-30, subject to funding/performance/approval  
**Planning volume:** approximately 980 unique participants; not a guaranteed minimum  
**Budget:** UNKNOWN. SCWDB says no final contract ceiling or target budget has been established; funding/volume depend on the Reskill DWG award, SCWDB allocation/subaward, participating workforce areas, negotiated pricing, and actual referrals.

## What the controlling public sources establish

SCWDB is procuring a coordinated program, not a single generic AI class. A responsive proposal should cover both services and all five Accelerator pathways: manufacturing, construction, logistics, healthcare, and business operations, plus AI-powered career readiness. Live remote is expected to be the routine delivery mode, with in-person capacity required for events such as layoffs or plant closures.

The published Q&A materially improves teaming feasibility:

- An out-of-state U.S. for-profit may serve as prime if it can meet delivery, administrative, financial, reporting, accessibility, security, and compliance obligations.
- Subcontractors and teaming partners are permitted for occupational-pathway specialization and local/in-person facilitation. The prime remains accountable for the whole contract.
- The organization/delivery team must show at least three years of relevant adult education, workforce training, career-readiness instruction, AI/digital-skills training, or closely related services.
- A recently formed prime may rely on clearly documented experience of key personnel, affiliates, or predecessors, with attribution stated accurately.
- Appendix C requires **three comparable references completed within the last five years**. References may include work by key personnel, affiliates, or predecessor entities if the performer, relationship, proposed-personnel role, and comparable scope are made explicit.
- Most importantly for this lane, SCWDB says a substantive experienced subcontractor/team member may help demonstrate the three-year qualification; the experienced organization does not necessarily have to be the prime. The prime still must demonstrate sufficient organizational, financial, administrative, management, and contract-management capacity.
- An existing LMS is not required at proposal time. Representative slides, activities, assessments, certificate/badge examples, and an instructor guide are required.
- No provider was selected or pre-awarded through the Reskill DWG application process.
- Separate one-time implementation/customization pricing and per-completed-participant pricing are permitted; milestone-based implementation invoicing may be proposed, subject to contract negotiation.

## Candidate teaming road: KCTCS Workforce Solutions / SKYCTC

**Candidate only — not contacted, not committed, not represented as a subcontractor.**

Kentucky Community and Technical College System (KCTCS) Workforce Solutions is a credible qualification target because public evidence shows long-running adult/workforce delivery at statewide scale, local South Central Kentucky capacity, named recent comparable engagements, and an emerging AI-training capability.

### Three-year experience — strongly evidenced

Public KCTCS sources report the TRAINS customized-workforce program operating since 2014, with 1,184 projects and 87,318 enrollments in its published lifetime statistics. A 2022 KCTCS workforce report described new-hire/incumbent-worker training and apprenticeship/work-based learning across manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, and business services. FY2024 reporting states KCTCS Workforce Solutions delivered 339 TRAINS projects and served more than 1,700 companies; SKYCTC alone reported customized training to more than 50 South Central Kentucky companies that year.

Evidence:

- https://workforce.kctcs.edu/trains.aspx
- https://kctcs.edu/newsroom/blog/posts/10102022-a-banner-year-for-training-the-workforce.aspx
- https://kctcs.edu/newsroom/news/2024/20240930-kctcs-reports-record-year-wf.aspx
- https://southcentral.kctcs.edu/news/2024/workforce-solutions-record-year.aspx

This clears a public-evidence screen for **duration and adult/workforce-training relevance**. It does not by itself secure the bidder's required reference contacts or proposed staff.

### Named comparable engagement 1 — YRCI / dislocated-worker career reskilling (2024)

Elizabethtown Community and Technical College Workforce Solutions, with Lincoln Trail Workforce Development Board, Kentucky Career Center — Lincoln Trail, and YRCI, delivered a 75-hour program preparing eight participants for full-time remote federal HR operations jobs. The public account describes employer co-design, job-entry outcomes, and workforce-board/on-the-job-training involvement.

Source: https://elizabethtown.kctcs.edu/news/2024/03-13-2024-wfs-yrci.aspx

**Why comparable:** adult workforce reskilling, employer-aligned instruction, career transition, remote-work readiness, workforce-board partnership.  
**What is still missing for Appendix C:** a consenting reference contact and confirmation that the engagement can be cited by the proposed KCTCS/SKYCTC team.

### Named comparable engagement 2 — BlueOval SK / large-scale manufacturing training (2024–2025)

ECTC's November 2025 board minutes report 626 BlueOval SK classes in FY2025, 1,673 students processed, 89 instructors, and a peak training day of roughly 340 students. KCTCS also publicly described the BlueOval SK training-center partnership in 2024.

Sources:

**Why comparable:** scaled instructor operations, manufacturing pathway, employer-driven workforce preparation, high-volume delivery.  
**What is still missing for Appendix C:** a consenting reference contact, precise services/dates attributable to the proposed delivery organization, and confirmation that the engagement may be used.

### Named comparable engagement 3 — Houchens Food Group / multi-year leadership training (2022–2025+)

SKYCTC reports that its Workforce Solutions division delivered customized leadership training to Houchens Food Group for **over three years**, totaling more than **2,000 training hours** to leaders across Houchens subsidiaries by July 2025.

Source: https://southcentral.kctcs.edu/news/2025/houchens-donation-to-pathfinder-pantry.aspx

**Why comparable:** directly local delivery organization, multi-year adult/business-operations training, customized employer curriculum, sustained service relationship.  
**What is still missing for Appendix C:** a consenting reference contact and confirmation of engagement details for proposal use.

### AI-specific evidence

KCTCS's general workforce depth should not be misrepresented as three years of AI training. Current public evidence is narrower:

- Bluegrass Community & Technical College Workforce Solutions explicitly lists **AI Training** among customizable professional-skills offerings: https://bluegrass.kctcs.edu/workforce-solutions/customized-training.aspx
- Jefferson Community & Technical College hosted and partnered on the January 2026 launch of the statewide **Democratizing AI Training and Access** workshop series led by the University of Kentucky Center for Applied Artificial Intelligence with the Kentucky Council on Postsecondary Education: https://jefferson.kctcs.edu/news/2026/20260202-jefferson-hosts-statewide-ai-workshop-series-kickoff.aspx

This is enough to treat KCTCS as an AI-capable **partner candidate**, not enough to claim it already has a fully production-ready curriculum covering SCWDB's five AI Industry Accelerator pathways.

## Why the lane is not BID yet

A public-source match is not a teaming agreement. Before a proposal can truthfully rely on KCTCS/SKYCTC, all of the following must be resolved:

1. **Partner consent and contracting entity:** identify whether KCTCS, SKYCTC, another KCTCS college, or a defined consortium is willing and legally able to participate as substantive subcontractor/teaming partner.
2. **Appendix C references:** obtain three consenting reference contacts for comparable work completed in the last five years and document the exact performer, relationship, proposed-personnel role, dates, scope, and outcomes.
3. **Instructor roster:** name actual instructors/SMEs with adult facilitation plus AI literacy and pathway experience. Do not substitute institutional reputation for named delivery capacity.
4. **All five pathway samples:** produce a representative lesson segment for manufacturing, construction, logistics, healthcare, and business operations, plus career readiness, with human review, fact-checking, privacy, security, employer-policy, and safe-use boundaries.
5. **Statewide deployment:** define remote cadence and realistic in-person response/travel coverage across participating Kentucky workforce regions.
6. **Prime capacity:** document the prime's legal authorization, management, financial/administrative capacity, reporting controls, privacy/security approach, debarment certifications, insurance road, and federal-grant compliance.
7. **Commercial model:** cost both one-time implementation/customization and per-completed-participant delivery using explicit cohort/travel/cancellation assumptions. The buyer has published **no final ceiling** and no guaranteed participant minimum.
8. **Data/accessibility:** define SCWDB-approved participant-record flow, accessibility review, attendance/completion evidence, restricted-data handling, retention, and audit support.

## Recommended division of labor if partner consent is later secured

**Prime / TJLabs-side contribution:** AI curriculum architecture; responsible-use guardrails; cross-pathway tool-selection policy; curriculum versioning; assessment/attendance/reporting automation; privacy/security design; implementation management; reusable instructor materials; measurement and continuous-improvement loop.

**KCTCS/SKYCTC candidate contribution:** substantive adult/workforce training experience; Kentucky instructor/deployment capacity; employer/workforce-board context; selected occupational-pathway instructors; local facilitation; eligible comparable references; training operations and participant-support practices.

This division is a planning hypothesis only and must not be represented externally as agreed.

## Decision

**HOLD — PARTNER-READY, NOT BID-READY.**

The qualification blocker that looked structural is tractable: SCWDB expressly allows a substantive experienced subcontractor to contribute qualifying experience, and KCTCS/SKYCTC has strong public evidence for the three-year adult/workforce requirement plus at least three recent named comparable engagements. The blocker has therefore narrowed from “find a plausible organization” to **secure a willing reference-bearing delivery partner and exact instructor/reference evidence**.

### Next authorized action

When owner authorization for partner outreach exists, send **one bounded teaming inquiry** to the appropriate KCTCS/SKYCTC Workforce Solutions business contact. Ask only:

- whether the organization is open to being a substantive teaming/subcontracting partner for this specific SCWDB RFP;
- whether it can provide three consenting comparable references from the last five years;
- whether it can name instructors/SMEs covering adult AI literacy plus the five occupational pathways and statewide/in-person delivery;
- which legal entity would contract and what proposal inputs/lead time it requires.

Do **not** submit to SCWDB or claim a partnership until those facts are confirmed. Because the proposal deadline is September 18, partner confirmation is the critical path.
