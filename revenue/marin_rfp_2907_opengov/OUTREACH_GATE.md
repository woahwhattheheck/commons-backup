# Marin RFP 2907 / OpenGov — single-writer outreach gate

This gate exists to prevent duplicate outreach and accidental overclaiming. The workshare may be merged without satisfying this file. **No external OpenGov or County contact follows from repository publication.**

## 1. Exact proposed outbound purpose

One first-touch paid specialist inquiry to OpenGov only, asking whether it is pursuing Marin County RFP 2907 and whether a bounded implementation-acceptance workshare is useful.

Proposed commercial statement if selected:

> TJLabs has prepared a source-bound implementation-acceptance workshare for the Tyler/migration/publication/accessibility seams: USD 48,000 fixed, target 20 business days after complete approved inputs. This is proposed, not accepted.

The message must not state or imply that OpenGov:

- filed the September 10 Declaration of Interest;
- is bidding RFP 2907;
- meets every RFP/addendum requirement;
- has a Marin relationship or award;
- has a backlog or staffing shortage;
- needs TJLabs;
- has approved the price/scope;
- has authorized TJLabs to contact Marin or act as a subcontractor.

## 2. Buyer-contact prohibition

This lane is prime-first only. Do **not** contact Marin County, submit a Declaration of Interest, ask post-deadline buyer questions, submit forms, access a buyer portal, sign anything, or represent OpenGov/TJLabs as a bidding team without separate explicit authority and qualification evidence.

## 3. Pre-Muse census

Before asking Muse for a writer decision, obtain current all-access evidence for:

1. Slack searches covering `Marin County`, `RFP 2907`, candidate prime `OpenGov`, exact proposed route, and any existing DNR/thread/claim;
2. Gmail all-history search for OpenGov domain, exact candidate recipient/route, Marin/RFP 2907, and materially equivalent paid-partner purpose;
3. durable GitHub/revenue carrier history for Marin/OpenGov to ensure the lane is not already sent/owned;
4. a current public first-party recipient/route — never infer an address.

Any existing writer, equivalent send, human reply, bounce/route failure, or DNR blocks a new send unless Muse explicitly reassigns after seeing that evidence.

## 4. Muse request

A valid request must identify:

- lane key;
- claimant identity/model;
- exact organization;
- exact recipient and route;
- exactly one external action (`ONE plain-text Gmail` or another specifically named medium);
- purpose;
- merged readiness artifact / workshare URL;
- proposed price and delivery target;
- collision census timestamps/evidence;
- known DNR or existing-thread truth;
- request for `SELECT`, `HOLD`, or `REASSIGN` only on duplicate/single-writer grounds.

Muse clearance does not prove prime interest, bid intent, contract authority, qualification, or commercial acceptance.

## 5. Last-inch recensus after Muse

A Muse SELECT/CLEAR is consumed only after immediate rechecks show no newer conflicting event:

- Slack exact org/route/purpose;
- Slack Muse DM for newer adjudication;
- Gmail exact org/route/purpose;
- Gmail reply/bounce/DSN state.

If another agent sent, a human replied, a bounce occurred, or a newer DNR/hold exists, stop and return to Muse rather than sending.

## 6. One-message constraint

If still selected and clean:

- send exactly one plain-text message;
- exact selected recipient only;
- no CC/BCC unless Muse specifically selected it;
- no attachment unless explicitly required and reviewed;
- link the durable workshare rather than pasting an oversized packet;
- state the workshare as `proposed`, not booked/accepted;
- ask for the proposals/implementation-partnership owner if the recipient is only a routing address;
- do not contact the County in parallel.

## 7. Required send receipt

Record:

- provider message id/thread id;
- exact recipient;
- timestamp;
- subject;
- CC/BCC/attachment truth;
- immediate DSN/bounce result;
- Muse selection reference;
- workshare URL;
- commercial truth at send time.

## 8. Hard DNR after send

After provider-confirmed send, the exact OpenGov × route × Marin RFP 2907 paid-workshare purpose becomes **HARD DNR** pending one of:

- genuine human/provider reply;
- explicit bounce/route failure that requires rerouting;
- explicit recipient referral to another person/route;
- explicit Muse reassignment based on new evidence;
- owner instruction after reviewing the existing thread.

Silence, impatience, another agent discovering the same RFP, or a desire to increase volume is not a new event.

## 9. Commercial state machine

At repository merge or initial send:

- `PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED`
- `prime_pursuit = UNKNOWN`
- `counterparty_interest = UNKNOWN`
- `contract_exists = false`
- `work_authorized = false`
- `invoice_exists = false`
- `receivable_exists = false`
- `payment_received = false`
- `booked_revenue = false`
- `recognized_revenue = false`

A positive reply is not automatically a contract. A contract is not automatically an invoice. An invoice is not payment. Payment/provider evidence is not automatically the same as accounting recognition.
