# Proposed TJLabs paid implementation/assurance workshare

Commercial hypothesis: $24,000 fixed / PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED.

This is an internal pricing and scope hypothesis for a qualified prime. It is not a bid, quote sent to a partner, accepted subcontract, receivable, invoice or revenue.

## Included workstream

### 1. Requirements-to-capability traceability

Map the literal MCC requirements to the prime-selected AI workspace and implementation approach. Unmet, ambiguous and prime-owned items remain visible rather than being papered over.

### 2. Institutional data / model-training controls

Produce a control matrix for institutional data handling, model/provider training use, retention, deletion, tenant isolation, provider changes, knowledge repositories, exports and administrator evidence.

### 3. Multi-model / assistant / workspace governance

Define practical governance profiles for model access, custom assistants, shared workspaces, role boundaries, knowledge sources, privileged tools, human review and lifecycle ownership.

### 4. Knowledge-ingestion acceptance tests

Create deterministic acceptance tests for document upload, indexing/search, source visibility, stale/replaced content, permission boundaries, citation/evidence behavior and deletion/retention.

### 5. SSO and LMS integration QA

Against prime-supplied interfaces and environments, provide a test plan and implementation QA for identity/role mapping, provisioning/deprovisioning, LMS launch/context boundaries, error handling and evidence capture.

### 6. Eight pilot templates

Deliver eight role-specific assistant/workspace pilot templates spanning faculty, students, administration and support use cases, with bounded authority and measurable acceptance criteria.

### 7. Admin telemetry and usage-governance pack

Define acceptance checks for admin dashboards, model/workspace adoption telemetry, access reviews, exception reporting, usage evidence and governance cadence.

### 8. Enablement

Administrator and end-user enablement materials plus two remote training sessions.

### 9. Pilot acceptance packet

Produce a deterministic acceptance/report packet for prime/buyer review, with requirement status, test evidence, unresolved risks, owner decisions and next actions.

## Pricing assumptions

The $24,000 hypothesis assumes:

- one qualified legal prime;
- one selected platform/product route;
- remote-first delivery;
- prime supplies required platform tenant, SSO/LMS interfaces and buyer coordination;
- up to eight pilot templates;
- two remote training sessions;
- one acceptance cycle plus one bounded correction cycle;
- no custom production connector build beyond the agreed SSO/LMS QA seam unless separately scoped.

## Exclusions

Not included or authorized:

- prime proposal administration or signatures;
- buyer or partner contact;
- Public Purchase account activity;
- legal, FERPA or accessibility opinions;
- insurance or indemnity commitments;
- license resale or platform fees;
- production data migration;
- penetration testing;
- unlimited custom integrations;
- help-desk operations after acceptance;
- live invoice/payment collection;
- award, payment, cash or recognized-revenue claims.

No free bespoke proposal drafting should begin before qualification and commercial alignment.
