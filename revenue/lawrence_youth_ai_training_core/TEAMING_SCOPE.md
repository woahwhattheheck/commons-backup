# Teaming Scope — Lawrence Youth AI Workforce Training

## Recommended commercial posture

Token Junkie Labs should participate only as a bounded **technical AI-training / evidence subcontractor** unless a qualified prime explicitly assumes the local workforce-program obligations and proposal authority.

## Qualified prime / local workforce provider owns

- bidder qualification, Massachusetts authorization, required references, and proposal submission;
- Lawrence youth recruitment, eligibility determination, enrollment, IEP/ISS or equivalent service planning, and intensive case management;
- participant consent, PII custody, accessibility/accommodation, supportive services, attendance policy, and any required safeguarding;
- local employer commitments, work-based-learning placements, internship/job placement, and 12-month follow-up;
- stipend and incentive administration, payroll/tax treatment, budget allowability, reimbursement evidence, and financial reporting;
- credential exam purchasing/registration and the authoritative determination that a credential was actually issued;
- performance reporting to the City/MMVWB, buyer communications, contract execution, insurance, and acceptance.

## Token Junkie Labs technical workstream can own

- versioned AI curriculum and instructor/lab package within the prime's approved delivery model;
- hands-on technical labs and deterministic assessment fixtures;
- responsible-AI, source/citation, privacy, data, cybersecurity, generative-AI, prompting, and workplace-AI modules;
- credential-preparation mapping for agreed credential tracks without claiming credential issuance;
- employer-defined technical project templates and acceptance-check schemas supplied by the prime/employer;
- deterministic technical-evidence compilation, replay verification, QA, and tamper-evident receipt generation;
- instructor-facing technical troubleshooting and evidence-quality support within the contracted scope;
- technical acceptance reporting back to the prime using opaque participant tokens rather than participant PII.

## Required inputs before a real delivery starts

1. Executed subcontract or written scope authorization from the prime.
2. Exact curriculum version and delivery calendar approved by the prime.
3. Prime-defined participant-token mapping kept outside this repository.
4. Approved credential track(s), exam-provider route, and who pays exam fees.
5. Employer-project requirements and authoritative acceptance-check set.
6. Prime's accessibility, safeguarding, privacy, retention, and incident procedures.
7. Agreed reporting interface and evidence-retention period.
8. Named human authority for scope changes and buyer-facing statements.

## Explicit non-authorities

This package cannot recruit or enroll youth, determine eligibility, issue credentials, approve stipends/incentives, place participants, contact the City on behalf of a prime, sign a proposal or contract, accept buyer terms, move funds, or recognize revenue. `TECHNICAL_TRAINING_EVIDENCE_READY` is evidence-only.
