## Proposed session outputs

Capture a short service/interface map, a trace of one completed change, a trace of one unresolved exception, and an evidence-gap log. Record the owner and version of each artifact and distinguish what was described from what was demonstrated. Resolve scope before requesting specialist evidence; clinical, laboratory, or compliance ownership is not presumed.

Do not derive a score from how many questions receive answers. A useful output is a reconstructable chain from observed context to a specific question, evidence inspected, uncertainty, and a proportionate improvement hypothesis. The prime and University retain responsibility for validating applicability and professional conclusions.
