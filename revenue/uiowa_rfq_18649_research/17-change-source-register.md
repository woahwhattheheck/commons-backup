## Date and claim rules

A copyright year, crawler timestamp, or access date is not a publication date. Undated records remain undated. A dated release note is evidence that the institution published an account of a change; it is not independent production verification. Benefits phrased as aims or expectations are not numeric outcomes. A live page can contain old or prospective language; current availability does not resolve that ambiguity.

For reuse, check whether the cited section still supports the exact claim, not merely whether the URL returns a page. Record a revised retrieval date if rechecked. Keep the earlier source date and the distinction between report, instruction, intent, and measurement. The source register is not a cryptographic archive and does not claim immutable page content.

## Retrieval exclusions and limitations

The following were not used as evidence: an inaccessible Indiana research-administration systems URL; a Michigan liaison-list request that returned a service error; and the UW August 11, 2026 release page that returned HTTP 403 to the research tool.
