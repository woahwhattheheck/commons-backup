## Recovery checklist

Before partner outreach or any readiness state beyond the present hold:

1. retain the exact buyer-hosted RFP bytes;
2. retain every current addendum/Q&A artifact;
3. compute SHA-256 and byte lengths from the retained files;
4. bind observed buyer-hosted URL and retrieval timestamp;
5. extract teaming/subcontract permission and any prime-only gates;
6. extract exact question/proposal deadlines and submission route;
7. extract evaluation/scoring, references, insurance/security and required forms;
8. extract authoritative current-system/objective/interface facts;
9. update `example_input.json` into a new authoritative generation;
10. compile with the packet SHA supplied out of band.

If public retrieval requires account creation, terms acceptance, buyer contact or another provider mutation, stop and record the boundary rather than bypassing it.
