# Proposed specialist workshare — internal commercial hypothesis

**Price hypothesis:** USD 24,000 fixed, `PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED`.

## Outcome

A bounded implementation-evidence sprint for a qualified investment-technology consulting/implementation prime. The sprint turns approved source/platform outputs into replayable evidence that systems agree where they are supposed to agree and expose explicit exceptions where they do not.

## Included

- one owner-approved system/domain authority map;
- up to eight admitted source/platform feeds or retained extracts;
- source-to-target field/domain mapping for the admitted scope;
- closed-period control totals and deterministic exception ledger;
- one position/accounting/performance parallel-run corpus if supplied by the prime/customer;
- retained evidence hashes and replay receipts;
- one corrected-data rerun;
- implementation/UAT handoff compatible with the generic Investment Operations Implementation Acceptance Desk once available.

## Excluded

- platform selection or procurement scoring;
- investment recommendations, asset allocation, trading or risk decisions;
- accounting policy or compliance certification;
- live custodian/broker/platform credentials;
- production mutation;
- data completeness/authenticity claims beyond retained evidence;
- TRS bidder status, references, insurance, certifications, forms, signatures or proposal submission;
- buyer acceptance, contract, payment, savings or revenue claims.

## Acceptance

The workshare is technically complete only when every admitted evidence generation is source-bound, every required control has a deterministic result, every unresolved exception is visible, and the complete package re-verifies from retained inputs. Business acceptance remains with the prime/customer.
