# GTRI qualification and capture strategy

## Decision

**Direct-prime bid: HOLD. Qualified-prime paid migration / lifecycle / integration acceptance workshare: PURSUE.**

This is not a product-platform bid from TJLabs. The public solicitation describes a full government-property system replacement with federal-property, security, accessibility, support/reliability, reporting and integration obligations. Current retained evidence does not establish that TJLabs owns a qualifying platform or satisfies those prime-bidder obligations.

The credible commercial seam is narrower and material: a qualified platform vendor / systems integrator can use TJLabs as a separate migration-quality control around legacy Sunflower data, asset life-to-date history, retained-system interfaces and UAT evidence.

## Why the workshare is sellable

The offer is not “we know federal property better than the prime.” The offer is:

> Freeze the prime's normalized migration contract and independent expected rosters. TJLabs reconciles the complete admitted Sunflower→target asset slice and migrated lifecycle-history slice, refuses silent scope contraction and duplicate identity, binds logical evidence roots, checks a bounded retained-interface roster, and returns an exception-first UAT evidence pack plus deterministic integrity receipt.

That creates commercial value even when the prime already has migration tooling because the acceptance evidence is generated by a separate control with explicit anti-contraction semantics. A migration that looks clean because both source and target extracts omitted the same asset is not accepted: the contract carries an independent expected asset roster. A history extract that omits the same event on both sides is not accepted: the contract carries an independent expected event roster.

## Prime-partner target archetypes

Prioritize firms that can independently satisfy the platform and federal-property procurement gates, for example:

1. **Federal property / asset management platform vendors** with demonstrated government-property implementations and migration capability.
2. **Systems integrators** experienced with Sunflower replacement and government property/accountability workflows.
3. **Enterprise asset / inventory vendors** able to integrate with Workday and Deltek Costpoint and willing to own the buyer's full platform/compliance response.
4. **Federal-property consulting / implementation practices** already partnered with a qualifying software platform and exposed to migration/UAT risk.

Do not target generic app shops merely because they can build CRUD screens. Do not represent TJLabs as a FAR/DFARS or NIST assessor.

## Paid wedge

Base hypothesis: **$28,000 fixed / PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED**.

Bounded unit:

- one frozen migration wave;
- up to **100,000 normalized asset-master rows**;
- up to **1,000,000 normalized migrated lifecycle-history rows**;
- one independently supplied expected asset roster;
- one independently supplied expected per-asset history-event roster;
- one frozen asset-field and history-field comparison contract;
- up to **four retained-enterprise interface** acceptance contracts;
- exception ledger, logical evidence roots, contract-bound receipt and one closeout review;
- target **15 business days** after complete intake and frozen criteria.

Optional: **$9,500 / PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED** cutover dress rehearsal for up to two replayed evidence sets against the same frozen contract.

These are negotiation hypotheses only, not accepted fees, invoices, receivables or recognized revenue.

## Free-to-paid path

The no-charge step is a **bounded fit check**, not production delivery:

- one 30-minute technical scoping call;
- review of one redacted/synthetic asset mapping excerpt and one lifecycle-history mapping excerpt;
- review of one retained-interface acceptance boundary;
- one-page fit/no-fit scope note.

Do not perform free production reconciliation, full migration QA, federal compliance analysis, proposal writing, live integration testing or cutover operations.

## Qualification questions for a prime

Before commercial commitment establish:

1. Are you bidding as software publisher, implementation prime, or subcontractor?
2. Which platform replaces Sunflower and who contractually owns migration quality?
3. Can you supply an independently frozen expected asset roster instead of deriving scope from the same extract being tested?
4. Can you supply a per-asset expected migrated-history roster or equivalent independent inventory of history events?
5. Which normalized source↔target fields must be invariant, and which transformations are intentionally allowed?
6. Are target platform-local asset IDs preserved or reminted with durable source lineage?
7. Which retained integrations are in UAT scope, and what constitutes `PASS` evidence for each?
8. Who owns FAR/DFARS/NIST/508/IUID/security/audit compliance conclusions? The answer must not silently transfer that authority to this workshare.
9. Can the work be executed on synthetic/redacted or contractually authorized non-production evidence under the prime's data-handling rules?
10. Do buyer terms permit the subcontract/workshare and required insurance/security posture?
