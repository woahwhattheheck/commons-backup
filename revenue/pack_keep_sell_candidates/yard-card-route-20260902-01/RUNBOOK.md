# Weekend yard-card route

A $100 KEEP vs SELL candidate. Print cards, walk one neighborhood, do
simple yard work for cash or an owner-pasted Payment Link. No ads from
this seat. Marketing stays with Bryce.

## What the operator keeps

- Card copy, price sheet, and invoice text in `assets/`
- This runbook
- A 50-door Saturday route they pick themselves

## What this pack does not include

- A Stripe URL. Leave checkout blank until Bryce pastes a live Payment Link.
- Ad spend, boosted posts, or an ad peer.
- Invented buyers, cash, or reviews.

## Saturday

1. Print 50 cards at a local shop. Black text on cheap card stock is enough.
2. Pick one neighborhood you can walk in two hours. Stay on public sidewalks.
3. Leave one card at each door. Do not put cards in mailboxes.
4. Write the street names and door count on paper. That is the route log.

## When a phone rings

1. Offer only the jobs on the price sheet.
2. Same-day windows: morning, midday, or late afternoon.
3. Collect the street address and a callback number. No account, no login.
4. Do the work. Take a before/after phone photo for the operator only.

## Getting paid

Cash or check on site is enough. If Bryce later pastes a live Payment Link
onto this candidate, use that exact URL and no other. Do not type a Stripe
address from memory.

## Sunday

1. Count cards left, doors walked, calls, booked stops, and money collected.
2. Post the counts in Slack `#business-packs` (`C0BU7JAPUH3`).
3. Leave `decision` as `UNDECIDED` until KEEP or SELL is written on the
   candidate. KEEP if three paid stops land in two weekends, or one stop
   books a repeat. SELL if the package is complete and the operator does
   not want the route.

## Cost to run one weekend

- 50 cards: about $12–$20
- Bags, gloves, a rake: about $20 if the operator does not already have them
- Time: two hours walking, plus job time

No paid ads. If someone wants ads, that is Bryce's lane.
