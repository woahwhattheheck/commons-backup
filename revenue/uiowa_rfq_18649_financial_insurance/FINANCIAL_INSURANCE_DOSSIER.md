## Related agreement and response decisions

RFQ Attribute 16 requires the selected Supplier to sign the UI-PSA and says its terms prevail during the resulting agreement; it expressly directs attention to insurance and liability. The supplied UI-PSA remains an uncompleted template, with the Contractor identity, assigned Contract number and other fields unfilled. Its opening states that it is not valid until signed by the University Purchasing Department and assigned a purchase order number. No executed agreement was located in this review.

The **Contractor Liability** paragraph requires, to the fullest extent allowed by law, indemnification and holding harmless of the **University of Iowa, State of Iowa, Iowa Board of Regents, and their agents and employees**, for claims or losses including reasonable attorneys' fees arising from negligence or omissions of the Contractor or its listed parties, including subcontractors, in providing products/services. This indemnity condition is distinct from the insurance additional-insured wording, which also names officers. Insurance limits do not establish an overall liability cap.

RFQ Attribute 15 requires proposed exceptions to the stated terms and conditions to accompany the proposal and says that providing none certifies compliance. Attribute 23 likewise requires certification of compliance except as expressly stated. The index records **no approved exception**; it does not select an acknowledgement or supply a claim of total compliance. The PSA's **ASSIGNMENT OR MODIFICATION** clause requires prior written mutual agreement for an assignment or modification; **CONFLICT OF TERMS** says its terms prevail over expressly conflicting Contractor agreement/proposal terms to the necessary extent.
