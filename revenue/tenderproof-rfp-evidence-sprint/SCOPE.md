# TenderProof RFP Evidence Sprint — fixed $2,500 pilot

## Commercial unit

One buyer, one solicitation, one fixed **$2,500 USD** pilot. Buyer-specific work starts only after an affirmative buyer response, written scope acceptance, and a verifiable funded payment rail. A positive lead or email reply is not payment and is not revenue.

## Buyer supplies

- the solicitation or authorized source bytes;
- a retained evidence register (capability statement, certifications, insurance, past-performance references, resumes, policies, contract vehicles, or other evidence the buyer is allowed to use);
- a point of contact for factual questions;
- explicit authorization to process the supplied materials for this pilot.

## Included deliverables

1. mandatory requirement and deadline matrix;
2. evidence binding for supported claims;
3. `BID`, `CONDITIONAL_BID`, or `NO_BID` gate with blockers;
4. missing-proof action queue;
5. evidence-linked response skeleton suitable for the buyer's proposal lead to edit;
6. deterministic input hashes and delivery receipt.

## Not included / never implied

- submitting the proposal or accepting solicitation terms;
- legal advice, legal review, or representation;
- inventing or certifying insurance, socioeconomic status, clearance, contract vehicles, past performance, customers, references, staff credentials, pricing, or security/compliance posture;
- buyer emails, portal actions, signatures, registrations, or procurement certifications unless separately and explicitly authorized;
- custom software integration, deployment, or ongoing proposal operations;
- automatic revenue recognition from a lead, yes, invoice, or even a buyer-entered payment flag. Accounting remains external.

## Completion acceptance

The pilot is complete when the agreed solicitation and evidence inputs deterministically reproduce the delivered matrix, gate, blockers/actions, response skeleton, hashes, and receipt, with unsupported assertions left visibly unclaimed.

## Expansion

Additional solicitations, evidence recovery, custom integrations, portal submission, or ongoing proposal operations are separate priced work/change orders. Do not silently extend the fixed pilot.
