**Commercial state:** `PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED`

**Counterparty:** a separately qualified prime, only after collision-safe route ownership and provider history are green.

## Deliverable

One fixed validation sprint that converts the prime's approved AI-IVR demo/integration claims into an evidence pack:

1. 25–40 mutually agreed synthetic or prime-approved de-identified municipal scenarios covering voice/chat/SMS/outbound paths actually in the prime's response.
2. Source-generation and citation contract for grounded answers/abstention.
3. Escalation and exactly-once effect tests, including timeout-after-commit ambiguity.
4. Selected multilingual variants supplied/approved by the prime; no linguistic-quality certification is invented.
5. Adapter/model/policy/input lineage commitments for each result.
6. Canonical machine-readable receipt plus human-readable claim→test→result matrix.
7. One rerun after a single bounded fix cycle.

## Binary acceptance

- every accepted scenario has exactly one terminal result;
- every grounded answer uses exactly the scenario-approved source generation and source refs;
- every prohibited/stale/conflicting knowledge case abstains, clarifies or escalates as contracted;
- every effect-bearing scenario has exactly one committed logical effect;
- exact replays add zero effects and conflicting same-ID generations fail closed;
- any provider-unknown + retry sequence is HOLD until reconciled;
- result/receipt/report independently verify byte-for-byte;
- all failed scenarios have machine-readable reasons and no failure is silently converted to PASS.

No prime qualification, five-reference requirement, 3-year government-platform experience, Cisco certification/configuration, Microsoft/Accela/ESRI production integration, SOC/PCI/security certification, translation certification, resident data, City portal action, proposal authoring as prime, signature, pricing form completion, award, production deployment, provider send, or payment collection is included unless separately contracted and truthfully evidenced.
