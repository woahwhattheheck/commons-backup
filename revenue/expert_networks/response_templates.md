# Expert-network response templates

Use these only inside the official GLG, Guidepoint, or Third Bridge expert
workflow. Replace bracketed placeholders privately before submitting. Do not
copy the completed response into Commons.

Never paste a client name, target company, raw screening question, project
brief, rate, provider reference, private link, or confidential detail into a
public record. State only experience that is accurate and already permitted to
be shared.

## Project-fit reply

```text
Thank you. My relevant experience is in [broad public expertise area], based on
[truthful non-confidential role category]. I can discuss public industry
practice and my permitted experience. I will not discuss my current employer,
confidential or proprietary information, material non-public information, or
anything restricted by an agreement or professional obligation. My conflict
check is [clear / requires provider review].
```

If the fit cannot be established without answering a restricted question, use
the decline template instead.

## Accept and schedule

```text
I can accept subject to the provider's final compliance clearance. My timezone
is [timezone]. I am available at [private option 1], [private option 2], or
[private option 3]. Please confirm the booked time, planned duration, official
connection method, and any recording or transcription consent in the provider
workflow.
```

Provider-specific timing:

- **GLG:** respond promptly, but do not state or calculate a numeric public
  response deadline; GLG's reviewed public expert pages publish none.
- **Guidepoint:** the current terms say accept or decline an invitation within
  24 hours and schedule an accepted project within five business days.
- **Third Bridge:** a reply within three hours is associated with higher
  selection likelihood; it is not a deadline or guarantee.

## Compliance decline

```text
I must decline this project because the subject may conflict with an
employment, contractual, fiduciary, regulatory, professional, or
confidentiality obligation. I will not provide the restricted information.
Please record this as a compliance decline in the official provider workflow.
```

The reason remains owner-private. Do not identify an employer, client, target,
agreement, or restricted topic in Commons.

## Stop during a consultation

```text
I need to stop this consultation because the discussion has reached a subject I
am not permitted to address. I will not answer that question or discuss the
restricted area. Please record the compliance stop and provide the official
closeout steps.
```

Provider-specific closeout facts:

- **GLG:** notify GLG; its public compliance page says an expert who terminates
  for a compliance issue will be paid for the time set aside.
- **Guidepoint:** notify Guidepoint immediately through its official channel;
  its terms allow a payment request for a full project ended early to comply
  with the terms.
- **Third Bridge:** terminate and notify Third Bridge immediately; its terms
  provide the applicable fee for actual time plus 15 minutes.

## Compensation confirmation

```text
Before I accept, please confirm the compensation and payment basis in the
official provider workflow, including whether payment is prorated to completed
minutes and the applicable payment-request or invoice deadline. I will not
disclose the platform's rate or financial arrangements outside that workflow.
```

Do not put a monetary amount in a Commons record.

## Post-consultation confirmation

```text
I confirm that the consultation was completed on [private date/time] and lasted
[private actual duration]. I have completed the provider's required closeout
and payment-request steps. No confidential or material non-public information
was intentionally provided.
```

Provider-specific timing:

- **GLG:** use the member workflow. The reviewed public pages publish no numeric
  expert-payment deadline.
- **Guidepoint:** submit the invoice within 45 days after completion. Payment is
  typically made within 15 days after the advisor requests it.
- **Third Bridge:** confirm the consultation within two business days. Payment
  is due within 30 days from the later of a valid payment request or invoice and
  client confirmation.

## Application or interest follow-up

```text
Hello. I registered interest on [private date] and am confirming that my
professional information remains current. I understand that selection depends
on client demand, topic fit, and compliance review. Please let me know through
the official provider workflow if further verification is required.
```

Do not imply a provider missed an application SLA: the reviewed public pages do
not publish an application-review deadline for GLG, Guidepoint, or Third Bridge.
Third Bridge describes its network as invite-only, so this follow-up is only for
an expert whom Third Bridge has already contacted; it is not a cold-application
template.
