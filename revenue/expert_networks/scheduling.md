# Expert-network scheduling and compliance

Checked against first-party public sources on 2026-08-26. This is an
operations guide, not legal advice. Provider terms and the instructions shown
inside the official provider workflow control when they differ from this file.

## Public-record boundary

Commons stores workflow metadata only. Do not put any of the following in this
public repository:

- a client identity, target company, raw screening question, project brief, or
  response body;
- an expert's name, contact details, precise availability, CV, employment
  history, conflict explanation, identity document, or background-check data;
- a rate, tax identifier, bank detail, invoice, provider token, session value,
  private portal URL, or receipt body.

Keep those values in the provider's official UI or an owner-private store. A
public record may retain a non-identifying status, timestamp, official source
URL, and SHA-256 of a private receipt.

## Before every application or project

1. Use only the provider's expert route described below.
2. Confirm that the professional profile is accurate and current.
3. Review employment, consulting, NDA, handbook, fiduciary, regulatory, and
   professional obligations.
4. Decline if the topic concerns a current employer, creates a competitor or
   other conflict, needs permission that has not been obtained, or could elicit
   confidential information or material non-public information (MNPI).
5. Accept terms, training, identity checks, or project attestations only in the
   official provider workflow.
6. Record only metadata allowed by the public-record boundary above.
7. Stop a consultation immediately if a conflict or restricted topic appears.

No provider guarantees a volume of work or selection for a project.

## GLG

### Route and profile

- Public expert application: <https://membership.glgresearch.com>, linked from
  <https://glg.com/for-experts>.
- GLG asks for work history and an accurate profile. Its public pages do not
  state a fixed years-of-experience minimum.
- GLG may perform identity, sanctions/watchlist, adverse-media, and, where
  required, criminal-record checks.

### Scheduling and payment

- GLG matches a profile to a project. If the client selects the expert, GLG
  finds a mutually available time for the engagement.
- The expert sets a rate in the GLG workflow. Payment follows a completed
  project and is based on that set rate.
- GLG says faster responses improve selection likelihood. It publishes no
  numeric application-response or project-response deadline on the reviewed
  public expert pages.
- The reviewed public pages publish no numeric expert-payment deadline. Do not
  infer one.

### Compliance

- Experts sign and annually re-sign GLG terms and complete annual interactive
  compliance training.
- Experts must review relevant agreements, decline conflicted projects, keep
  client interests and information confidential, and never disclose
  confidential information or MNPI.
- Employed experts may not consult about their employer. GLG says written
  employer consent may be required for written, in-depth, or ongoing work.
- GLG says an expert who stops and reports a consultation for a compliance
  reason will be paid for the time set aside.

- <https://glg.com/compliance>

## Guidepoint

### Route and profile

- Public expert-interest form: <https://www.guidepoint.com/experts/>.
- The public form asks for name, phone, and email; it also offers LinkedIn and
  message fields. These values are owner-private and must not be copied here.
- Guidepoint asks prospective advisors on that route to have at least 10 years
  of experience. A fit may lead to an invitation and verification/compliance
  steps; registering interest is not acceptance into the network.
- Guidepoint requires accurate employment, education, conflict, and restriction
  information and may verify professional and educational history and perform
  background checks.

### Exact provider timing

These Guidepoint timings apply only to Guidepoint:

- The current advisor terms say an invitation should be accepted or declined
  within **24 hours**.
- An accepted project should be scheduled within **five business days** of the
  invitation.
- The expert page says consultations are often completed within **72 hours of
  the initial request**. That is a descriptive project cadence, not an
  application-review promise.
- An advisor invoice must be submitted within **45 days after project
  completion**.
- Payment is typically made within **15 days after the advisor requests
  payment** through the advisor website.

### Payment and compliance

- Compensation is the customary consulting fee agreed by the advisor and
  Guidepoint. A consultation is normally prorated to full completed minutes;
  the client controls actual duration, and a consultation that does not occur
  is not paid unless Guidepoint agrees otherwise in writing.
- Rate changes require written notice and Guidepoint confirmation, affect only
  future projects, and may not be disclosed to clients without written consent.
- An invitation provides scope, expected time, and compensation in the provider
  workflow. The advisor may accept or pass without a minimum-project
  obligation or penalty. Screening responses are not compensated.
- Advisors complete verification, a compliance tutorial on joining and at least
  every 12 months before projects, and project-specific attestations. They must
  decline or stop work that would create a conflict or disclose confidential
  information or MNPI.

## Third Bridge

### Route and profile

- Third Bridge describes its expert network as **invite-only** and says it
  contacts an expert directly when a relevant, experience-matched,
  conflict-free project exists: <https://www.thirdbridge.com/en-us/experts>.
- Expert Hub is for existing/invited experts. Do not use the client "Request a
  trial" route or a generic business contact form as an expert application.
- Third Bridge custom-sources and background-checks new experts, validates
  relevant knowledge and experience, and asks experts to verify identity and
  keep biographical and employment information current.
- Its reviewed public expert pages state no fixed years-of-experience minimum.

### Exact provider timing

These Third Bridge timings apply only to Third Bridge:

- The expert page says experts who respond to an opportunity within **three
  hours** are twice as likely to be selected. This is a selection correlation,
  not a response deadline or guarantee.
- A typical engagement is a one-hour direct phone call, but actual engagements
  can differ and may include surveys, panels, or advisory work.
- Within **two business days after a consultation**, the expert must confirm it
  occurred and, when paid, apply through the web application with the actual
  duration, agreed rate, and bank destination.
- Payment for a completed consultation is due within **30 days from the later
  of** a valid payment application or invoice and the client's confirmation
  that the consultation occurred.
- A non-payment query must be raised within **six months after the end of that
  30-day period**.

### Payment and compliance

- Completed consultations are paid at the agreed rate, prorated by actual
  minutes. A consultation that does not occur is not paid.
- If an expert terminates early for a compliance reason, the terms provide the
  applicable fee for actual time plus 15 minutes.
- Experts act in a personal capacity, keep client identity, consultation
  subject/questions, and agreed rates confidential, and must not disclose
  confidential information, MNPI, or trade secrets.
- Experts must not consult principally about a current employer, provide
  employer-proprietary information, knowingly consult for a current employer's
  competitor, or violate a legal, contractual, professional, regulatory, or
  ethical obligation. A discovered conflict requires immediate termination and
  notice to Third Bridge.
