Attachment L separately asks about AI in the solution and AI used by developers, including disablement and four-year plans. It supplies no-use/no-plans response routes; no blanket AI prohibition is inferred.

A vendor-cloud exception requires independently verified NIST 800-53 compliance or a detailed independently verified path. The incorporated policy, sample contract and cloud terms were not substantively reviewed here, so this packet does not claim complete security, legal or contract readiness.
