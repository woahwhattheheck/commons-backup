1.0	Technical Proposal Questions
	1.1 Company Background
1.1.1 Provide an overview of your organization, including its history, mission, and core competencies relevant to statewide program modernization, human services operations, or Medicaid Administrative Claiming.

	1.3 Time Study Tools and Methodology
1.3.1 Describe your methodology for redesigning, implementing, and piloting the updated MAC time study tools.
1.3.2 Explain how your organization will collaborate with AAAs and FSSA during pilot phases.
	
1.4 Cost Reporting Processes
1.4.1 Describe your approach to developing standardized cost reporting templates, cost pooling processes, and guidance tools.
1.5 AAA Monitoring and Quality Improvement

1.6.2 Describe your approach to stakeholder support, including AAAs and other participating partners.
1.7 Onboarding Additional Agencies
1.7.1 Describe your approach to onboarding new agencies into the MAC program upon FSSA direction.
1.8 Long‑Term Sustainability Preparation

1.9 Deliverables and Reporting

1.11 Remedies for Non-Performance
