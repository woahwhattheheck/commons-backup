Creator brief
The reference workflow plans per-kit materials and fixed workshop supplies. Demand notes are operator-provided, not independently verified. Replacing the planner with another workflow requires implementation, not just a new label.


Targets are visible guidance, not identity or payment restrictions. Generated apps save plans locally and contain no billing integration.


In material defaults, set quantity_basis to per_workshop for a quantity used once, or per_kit for each attendee, demonstration and spare kit. Omitted values keep the original per-kit behavior. The existing per_attendee field holds the quantity for the chosen basis; reserve and whole-pack rounding apply to both.

What goes into the handoff?
A standalone planner, exact brief, saved revisions, onboarding and support instructions, draft launch copy, and file checksums. No deployment, sale, newsletter send or customer installation happens automatically.
The studio stores briefs in its SQLite file; the generated planner stores plans in the end user's browser. Exported JSON moves saved plans between browsers. Keep backups before clearing browser data.
