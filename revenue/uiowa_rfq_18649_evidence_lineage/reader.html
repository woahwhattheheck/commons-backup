How to read this demonstration
Worked example: case 05 → case 06. The historical finding cites A. Two revision paths end at C and D. In case 05, only C is supplied; D remains visible as omitted. In case 06, D arrives. Both drafts can now be inspected, but the report still says BRANCHED_SUCCESSION_REVIEW. The reviewer must decide whether these are competing revisions or valid parallel scopes.
Then case 07. E explicitly references both C and D by record identity and digest. This supplies a convergence declaration. It does not authenticate an approval or settle the finding's substantive review.
