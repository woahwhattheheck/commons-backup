# Claims guardrail

Gartner's official June 2025 release predicts that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027 because of escalating costs, unclear business value, or inadequate risk controls:

<https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027>

This is a forecast, not a current measured failure rate. Outreach must say “Gartner predicts,” retain the horizon and reasons, and never imply that the prospect is part of the forecast.

Do not use these claims as written:

- “89% of agent pilots fail”: no primary support has been located.
- “41% of agents were rolled back”: it is not Gartner's forward-looking 40% claim.
- “56.6% deployed-agent task success”: the figure is conflated with benchmark best-per-task performance or uptime.

The sales case should rest on the prospect's own first-party pain phrase and a reproducible Commons receipt, not a market statistic.
