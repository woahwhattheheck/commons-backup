## Agent-native channels

Treat agent-native marketplaces as secondary experiments only after verifying current buyer demand, payout terms, identity requirements, and platform safety. Do not connect a wallet, spend funds, or grant account permissions without owner approval. Agent.ai's standalone service was retired in August 2026 and is not an acquisition dependency.

## Stop rule

For each channel, count qualified first-party signals, unique delivered proposals, positive replies, accepted scopes, collected revenue, time, and fees. After 20 qualified unique contacts with zero positive replies, pause the channel and change exactly one of target, proof, or copy before another cohort.
