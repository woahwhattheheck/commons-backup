# Pain-triggered outreach

This playbook sells the already-published USD 2,500 Same-Day Agent Survival Proof. It does not create another SKU and does not alter the independent seven-contact GGUF transport.

## Qualifying language

Contact a prospect only when the prospect's own current, first-party source contains an observable production failure such as:

- “works in the demo” or “works locally” but fails in production;
- session drop, disconnect, stuck run, timeout, or retry loop;
- duplicate job, double-send, or repeated external action;
- state lost after a tool or API failure;
- partial tool execution requires human cleanup;
- no replayable audit trail or no way to reproduce the failure;
- rollback cannot restore an external side effect;
- a regression reached customers before the evaluation caught it.

Record the exact phrase and source URL. A broad “AI” or “agent” label is not a pain signal.

## Channel order

1. Answer through the route attached to the voiced pain: an active marketplace job, company-owned issue/discussion, public founder post, or named engineering contact.
2. Use direct email only when a first-party trigger and relevant role are recorded. Do not blast generic inboxes.
3. Point to the commit-pinned Commons receipt, not a claim that Commons fixed the prospect's private system.
4. Publish each completed public-safe proof so subsequent outreach has closer evidence.

Respect channel rules and opt-outs. Never scrape behind access controls.

## Follow-ups

At most two follow-ups are permitted without a reply.

**Three business days later**

> The scope is deliberately small: one sentence, one pre-agreed binary test, one public-safe proof and receipt. Is “[pain phrase]” still active, or should I close this?

**Seven business days after that**

> Closing the loop. If “[pain phrase]” returns, send the one sentence and I will confirm fit before any invoice.

A negative reply or opt-out is do-not-contact immediately. Scheduled messages are not failed sends and must not be duplicated to bypass a mailbox limit.
