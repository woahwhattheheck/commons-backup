# Start here: cross-group synthesis review

**UIOWA-028 — fictional evidence, usable assessment preparation.**

This reader pack is usable without running development tools. It contains a
[complete synthesis method](28-synthesis-method.md) and a
[worked table with the supporting and dissenting source excerpts](examples/themes.md).
The examples are invented, not University findings, peer rankings, evidence of
contract acceptance, or a statement about any employee.

## What to inspect first

Open the worked table's **business-recovery-check** theme. Its three group labels
refer to the reach of one shared recovery exercise. They do not triple the source
count or create independent corroboration. IAM's counterexample remains attached
to the same theme, which is marked contested and calls for focused follow-up.

Then compare the two **delivery-wait** themes. Both fictional traces describe
120 minutes of waiting, but the supplied explanations differ: one concerns
handoff ownership, the other a busy test worker. A single generic “speed up
releases” recommendation would hide the actionable difference.

Finally, inspect **requirements-review**. A manual ESS checklist and automated
RIS routing support the same selected review outcome. The method preserves their
implementation context rather than rewarding automation as a maturity level.
IAM has no relevant sample, so its result is unknown, not poor performance.

## A worked decision conversation

| Reviewer question | Answer supported by this packet | Next useful evidence or decision |
|---|---|---|
| Does the recovery gap affect every service in all three groups? | Not established. The supplied finding annotations reach three groups, but there is no complete service population or sampling frame. | Identify the affected service universe and test representative business functions and dependencies. |
| Does IAM's successful sign-in prove the shared recovery concern wrong? | No. It is a counterexample whose scope must be compared with the original exercise; it does not establish registration or research recovery. | Reconcile exercise boundaries, versions and business checks with the shared owner and IAM lead. |
| Should all groups use the automated review workflow? | These examples do not justify that conclusion. The sampled manual and automated practices meet the selected outcome. | Compare workload fit, error handling, support effort and adoption cost before prescribing a common mechanism. |
| Are the two delivery waits one department-wide problem? | The symptom is similar, but the supplied mechanisms differ. Two linked local actions are more faithful to the evidence. | Validate each explanation in an appropriate local sample; retain the shared symptom as comparison context. |
| Is the AI-review policy an established operating strength? | The policy supports an intended practice, not demonstrated use. The theme remains unresolved and reported. | Obtain an actual use example and its review record, or establish that the workflow is outside scope. |

## Relation to the already published writing pack

The [UIOWA-083 worked narratives](../uiowa_rfq_18649_themes/worked_narratives.md)
are complementary reader-facing examples, not the same dataset. That pack's first
scenario records 18 incidents in one shared register, partitioned into 8 ESS,
6 RIS and 4 IAM cases. The 18 cases must not become 54 by recounting the register
for each group. Its IAM participant account qualifies notification coverage
without erasing the recorded initial-ownership strength.

Do not pool those incidents with this pack's fictional recovery or review cases.
The writing pack explicitly keeps its three scenarios separate. Source and finding
IDs need their document/scenario namespace before cross-component reconciliation.
The writing pack uses relative exercise boundaries such as E01–E28; the current
synthesis engine's input requires ISO calendar windows. An automatic adapter must
not invent calendar dates, service scope, source independence or causal support to
bridge that difference. Keep the original boundary until an explicit, justified
mapping is available.

## Review disposition

A reviewer can accept this **method as a proposed preparation approach** without
accepting its fictional findings as real facts. Record any changes to scope,
comparison rules or wording with the affected theme and source IDs. A shared-owner
role is a proposed responsibility, not a named person's commitment or a meeting.
All calendar and delivery decisions remain outside this reader pack.
