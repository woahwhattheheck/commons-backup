# Proposal assembly skeleton

This is an assembly/control map, not proposal prose.

## Volume 1 — Corporate Information (maximum 4 pages)

Only owner-approved facts: legal offeror identity/address, requested corporate structure/status, authorized point of contact, responsibility/financial/organizational facts actually requested and proven, and deviations/exceptions if any.

**Never infer** registration, insurance, certifications, financial capacity, or other bidder status from repository history.

## Volume 2 — Technical (maximum 12 pages, resumes excluded)

1. Executive approach and source-to-deliverable traceability.
2. Current-state assessment method.
3. Governance / target operating model.
4. Three-year roadmap method.
5. Agentic-AI evaluation method.
6. Pilot recommendation method.
7. Project management, meetings, acceptance and change control.
8. Capabilities actually available to the offeror/team.
9. Named key personnel and role allocation.
10. AI-use disclosure: no-AI scenario; AI-assisted scenario with each use/circumstance described; explicit statement that actual AI use requires prior written USAC approval.

Avoid requirement restatement without method; the RFP says that is technically unacceptable.

## Volume 3 — Experience & Past Performance (maximum 5 pages)

For each of 2–3 current/recent contracts actually held by prime or proposed team member:
- client name;
- project title;
- period of performance;
- contract/order/engagement identifier;
- contract value;
- primary reference contact and backup/explanation;
- one-page maximum relevance narrative;
- problems/corrective actions and results, if applicable.

Reference availability/permission should be confirmed before inclusion because USAC may email questionnaires and the offeror is responsible for completion.

## Volume 4 — Price (maximum 4 pages)

Use the **official Attachment 1 Bid Sheet**. Fully loaded FFP only. If AI-assisted performance is proposed, preserve separate with-AI and without-AI bid-sheet scenarios as requested by the RFP. Do not invent a market-clearing price before the actual team/LOE is known.

## Separate attachment controls

- signed USAC Confidentiality Agreement;
- official Bid Sheet (Excel may accompany Volume 4);
- resumes: <=2 pages each for named key personnel;
- single submission email to the official procurement addresses.

## Final pre-submission fence

1. Re-open USAC official procurement page.
2. Re-fetch RFP, Q&A, Bid Sheet, Confidentiality Agreement and any addenda.
3. Hash exact bytes and compare source generation against the compliance matrix.
4. Re-run qualification with owner-approved bidder/team facts.
5. Validate page limits and PDF presentation rules.
6. Confirm references are reachable.
7. Confirm authorized signer/submission owner.
8. Only that authorized human sends the proposal.
