# Technical approach architecture — source-mapped, not a proposal claim

This architecture is a working design for Volume 2 if and only if an authorized offeror decides to pursue IT-26-139. It does not claim that any named company has performed this work.

## Workstream 1 — current-state evidence map

Build a traceable inventory of existing USAC AI strategy, policies, standards, governance committees, risk inventory, vendor/software review process, Microsoft Copilot rollout, regulatory/privacy/security obligations, organizational decision rights, workforce readiness, data and technology readiness.

Output: evidence register with `observed fact / source / owner / control gap / decision impact`, plus interview map and contradiction log.

## Workstream 2 — governance and operating model

Turn current-state findings into a target operating model:

- governing bodies and decision rights;
- intake, approval, exception and escalation paths;
- model/tool/vendor inventory;
- control objectives and evidence expectations;
- executive/Board reporting;
- privacy/security/compliance interfaces;
- value/cost measurement and program-line allocation.

Each recommended control should map back to a current-state finding and forward to a roadmap decision gate.

## Workstream 3 — three-year roadmap

Sequence initiatives with dependencies, prerequisites, decision gates, indicative resourcing and cost tracking. Keep three lanes distinct:

1. traditional/statistical AI;
2. internal generative-AI efficiency;
3. business-process/customer-experience AI, including exploratory agentic AI.

Do not present agentic AI as a predetermined target. Evaluate oversight, output validation, monitoring, failure modes and consumption-based cost as explicit decision variables, matching the RFP's exploratory posture.

## Workstream 4 — pilot recommendation and executive readout

Score candidate pilots against mission value, current-state traceability, data/technology readiness, privacy/security/compliance fit, change/workforce readiness, measurable success criteria, cost/operational ownership, and reversibility/failure containment.

Recommend one executable pilot **plan**, not an implementation. The contract scope explicitly excludes building/deploying/operating/modifying capabilities.

## AI-use boundary

Two delivery scenarios must remain separately costable:

- **No-AI performance** — all engagement work performed without AI tools.
- **AI-assisted performance** — proposal describes each intended AI use and separately prices it; actual use remains blocked until prior written USAC approval.

No internal carrier should silently assume AI use, upload USAC material to an external service, or treat proposal-time disclosure as performance-time approval.
