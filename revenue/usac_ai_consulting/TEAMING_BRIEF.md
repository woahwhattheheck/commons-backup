# Prime vs teaming decision brief

## Why teaming is a first-class route

IT-26-139 does not merely ask for individual expertise. Volume 3 asks for two to three recent similar **corporate** contracts and reachable references. USAC's Q&A clarifies that experience gained by a person at a prior employer may appear in a resume but is not corporate past performance for evaluation. Qualifying contracts held by a proposed teaming partner/subcontractor may be used.

Therefore:

- do not turn a strong resume into a fake company reference;
- do not list a client without permission and a reachable reference;
- do not claim a contract was held by the prime if it was not;
- do not bid as prime merely because the technical approach is strong.

## Minimum partner contribution for the past-performance route

A proposed teammate should contribute enough real evidence to close the corporate past-performance gate:

1. contract held by that organization;
2. performance date inside the RFP's three-year recency window;
3. similar enterprise-AI strategy/governance scope;
4. regulated environment with federal oversight, funding, or reporting obligations comparable in character to USAC;
5. client/project/period/contract reference/value fields available as required;
6. primary reference contact reachable and permission/availability confirmed;
7. willingness to be named as a teaming partner/subcontractor and to support USAC's reference questionnaire.

A partner can also supply named key personnel, including the AI SME, provided the proposed relationship is real and the named personnel remain committed.

## What this carrier does not authorize

No cold representation to USAC that a partner is committed. No partner's name, contracts, values, client contacts, rates, certifications, or credentials may enter a proposal until supplied/approved by the relevant organization.

## Decision

Run `qualification.py` with real owner-approved facts.

- `PRIME_READY`: prime itself proves >=2 qualifying recent corporate contracts.
- `TEAMING_REQUIRED`: prime does not, but real partner evidence can reach the floor.
- `HOLD`: source, identity, US-performance, key-personnel, deadline, or past-performance path remains unproven.

`PRIME_READY` is not submission authority. It only means the bidder-path gate is evidence-complete.
