# Prime / teaming decision brief

No direct-bid eligibility claim is made. The official package determines whether Token Junkie Labs can bid directly, should team, or should no-bid.

## Direct path — only if source-proven

Consider prime response only after all mandatory corporate, jurisdictional, insurance, reference, staffing, security/privacy, financial, and procurement requirements are extracted and truthfully evidenced.

## Teaming path

If the technical Archives-AI PoC fits but mandatory institutional/procurement qualifications do not, prefer a qualified prime or archive/library/records partner while preserving a bounded technical role. Potential specialist contributions, subject to the actual RFP, include:

- provenance/citation and source-version custody;
- deterministic evaluation/replay/change-control tooling;
- retrieval/abstention/human-review acceptance harnesses;
- reproducible AI experiment packaging;
- technical integration and evidence automation.

A prospective organization is not a partner until an actual agreement exists.

## Questions that controlling sources must answer before outreach

- Are consortiums/subcontractors permitted and how are their qualifications scored?
- What institutional, archive/library, public-sector, AI, data, security/privacy, or consulting references are mandatory?
- Are Canadian/Manitoba registration, insurance, local-presence, tax, workers-compensation, or security requirements mandatory?
- Can subcontractor/key-person experience satisfy bidder qualifications?
- Is any University data permitted in external cloud/API/AI systems?
- What bidder questions may be asked, through which official channel, and by what deadline?
- Are demos, interviews, presentations, or negotiations mandatory?

Until these are source-bound, teaming posture stays exploratory and the pursuit remains on HOLD.
