## Why the workshare is credible

The narrow product is not “we will implement your ERP.” It is:

> Give TJLabs the prime's frozen migration mapping, bounded source/target extracts, and retained-interface acceptance criteria. We independently reconcile the migration wave, refuse ambiguous duplicates and silent field loss, bind each of the six migration phases to evidence, record interface exceptions, and return a deterministic acceptance receipt plus human-readable exception ledger.

That work has direct commercial value to an ERP prime because it isolates migration/cutover assurance from platform licensing and core implementation. It also creates an explicit evidence boundary around one of the highest-risk parts of a legacy ERP replacement: whether transformed records and retained interfaces are actually what the agreed mapping says they should be.

## Prime-partner target archetypes

Prioritize firms that can independently satisfy the buyer's actual platform/procurement gates and already have one of these shapes:

1. **Lawson/Infor replacement specialists** with cloud-ERP implementation references.
2. **Cloud ERP implementation partners** carrying required security, insurance, and public/nonprofit references.
3. **Maryland-capable primes** that need additional MBE/subcontract participation or independent migration QA, but never represent TJLabs as certified MBE unless verified.
4. **Systems integrators** retaining Dayforce, recreation/membership, Smartsheet or IVR around the new ERP and therefore exposed to interface/cutover reconciliation risk.

Do not target generic software shops that cannot name a qualifying ERP platform or comparable implementation record.

## Paid wedge

Base hypothesis: **$24,000 fixed / PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED**.

Bounded unit:

- one agreed migration wave;
- up to 250,000 normalized source/target records supplied by the prime;
- one frozen field/transform contract;
- up to three retained-system interface acceptance contracts;
- exception ledger, evidence map, deterministic receipt, and one closeout review;
- 15 business days after complete intake and frozen acceptance criteria.

Optional: **$8,000 / PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED** cutover dress-rehearsal extension for up to two replayed migration/interface evidence sets against the same frozen contract.

The price is a negotiation anchor, not an accepted fee, invoice, receivable, or revenue figure.

## Free-to-paid path

A prime can receive a no-charge **fit check**, not free delivery:

- 30-minute technical scoping call;
- review of one redacted mapping excerpt and one interface boundary;
- one-page go/no-go summary explaining whether the bounded workshare can be priced safely.

No free production reconciliation, full mapping audit, cutover execution, or buyer deliverable. The free step exists only to establish a path to the paid sprint.

## First contact questions

Before offering scope, establish:

1. Are you pursuing RFP 27-05 as prime, implementation partner, or ERP publisher?
2. Which ERP platform and implementation entity own migration responsibility?
3. Is independent migration reconciliation / cutover evidence already staffed?
4. Which retained interfaces create the highest cutover risk?
5. Can the prime provide a frozen mapping contract plus redacted/synthetic source-target samples before commercial commitment?
6. Would independent exception evidence be included under the prime's QA plan or held as an internal readiness gate?
7. Are subcontracting, certification, MBE, insurance, data-handling, and security terms compatible with a bounded specialist workshare?
