placeholders, not evidence. Remove instructions only after completing the source map.

## Reader-facing narrative

**[Concrete headline]**

[Sentence 1: common condition and the scope in which it is observed. Cite finding
IDs. Sentence 2: the meaningful differences or exceptions. Cite those findings.]

[Sentence 3: why the condition matters for service outcomes. Mark a prospective
consequence as a hypothesis unless observed evidence establishes it. Sentence 4:
the practical department response, with group-specific action where necessary.]

[Final sentence: the most important uncertainty, dissent or decision dependency.]

Target a short narrative that can stand in the report with its traceability table.
Do not conceal an unresolved material disagreement in an appendix-only note.

is a proposed measure; neither is an observed result or a resource commitment.

## Author's completion prompts

- Can each factual clause be followed to an exact passage without searching for a
  document by title alone?
- Is the department proposition supported at the stated scope, and are shared
  records counted once?
- Are effective local alternatives, contradictory evidence and missing information
  visible in the narrative?
- Does the response address the observed practice and name a responsible role,
  realistic effort assumption and observable result?
- Would a new source that changes a linked finding trigger a visible revision?
- Are fictional examples and proposed plans unmistakably different from observed
  University facts and confirmed commitments?
