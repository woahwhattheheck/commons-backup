# RFQ 18649 editable coordination forms

Preparation templates for UIOWA-008. All assignments, participant choices, sessions, and review windows remain proposed until the accountable parties agree them. Populate identifiable people and confidential evidence only in the engagement's approved private copy. These forms create no invitation or appointment.

Use the [mobilization guide](README.md) for the kickoff agenda, first-week plan, relative delivery calendar, source register, and decision log. The editable workbook presents the same workflows with input cells, name-entry counts, and duplicate flags. The RFQ is the authority for the role descriptions; the table layout and identifiers are new operating designs.
