# Commons offering families

Bryce's source directives: Slack `1787779119.206119`, `1787779125.190459`, and `1787779142.155719` on 2026-08-26.

Commons sells and develops across four first-class families at once: **products, services, expertise, and data**. No family replaces another. Each composes with the existing commercial ladder and every shipped or paid claim still requires a dated receipt.

## Products

Packaged things a buyer can receive, run, keep, or license.

Current product surfaces include TITAN Hands, Commons MCP deployments, Muhlnickel/Titan keep-or-build, crash-resume proof artifacts, and reproducible receipt tooling. Productization means a named deliverable, version or source pin, transfer boundary, and verification evidence.

Next expansion: attach every product-capable repository artifact to an existing or new catalog listing without removing the present SKU ladder.

## Services

Bounded work performed for a buyer.

Current services include the $2,500 same-day crash-resume proof, $15,000 recovery sprint, $12,000 GGUF diagnostic, $30,000 White Box pilot, $250 White Box hour, and private agent/tool integration. A service receipt records scope, delivery evidence, and measured outcome without inventing acceptance or cash.

Next expansion: route each demonstrated Commons capability into at least one bounded service deliverable.

## Expertise

Judgment, diagnosis, design, review, and teaching sold independently of a software transfer.

Offerable expertise includes agent architecture review, model/GGUF diagnosis, failure and recovery analysis, computer-use design, reproducibility review, evidence architecture, carrier/resource routing, and Muhlnickel/Titan technical consultation. Expertise can be delivered as an advisory hour, written assessment, design review, or embedded engagement.

Buyer surface: [`expertise.html`](../expertise.html) exposes the eight named advisory lanes, while the canonical evidence-bound compiler/verifier remains [`revenue/expertise_catalog/`](./expertise_catalog/). The existing source-backed White Box hour stays `$250/hour` through its canonical commerce route; the other seven lanes remain quote-only until buyer-specific price and terms are recorded. The buyer surface and compiler do not themselves claim a buyer, acceptance, delivery, settlement, payout, or cash.

## Data

Datasets, benchmark results, inventories, corpora, manifests, and reproducible evidence packages that can be transferred or licensed.

Candidate data surfaces include the scrubbed public receipt corpus, benchmark outputs, reproducibility manifests, and the White Box research archive. Data becomes offerable only to the extent its checksum, provenance, license, sensitive-data class, redaction state, and transfer boundary are recorded. Unknown remains `UNKNOWN`; no unverified archive or customer material is published.

Next expansion: complete the archive inventory and create redacted sample packs plus explicit dataset/license offers where the recorded rights permit them.

## Composition rule

A single engagement may include all four families:

1. a **product** the buyer keeps;
2. a **service** that installs, adapts, or proves it;
3. **expertise** that diagnoses and designs the system;
4. **data** that benchmarks, documents, or trains/evaluates it.

Catalog and outreach work should name the family or families explicitly. Do not treat a zero measurement as permission to remove an offer. Record traffic, reply, acceptance, delivery, transfer, and cash separately.

The accepted-scope-to-delivery composer (`revenue/scope_to_delivery/`, `scope-to-delivery.html`) turns a written agreement in any family into those measured artifacts without inventing the sale.
