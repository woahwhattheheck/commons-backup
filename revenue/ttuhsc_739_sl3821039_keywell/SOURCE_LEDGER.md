## Reconciled discrepancy

One third-party page reports a September 14 question deadline and a mandatory August 26 pre-bid meeting. The recovered controlling RFP text states the **written-question deadline is August 21, 2026** and the key-events schedule shown in the base RFP does not establish that pre-bid fact. Therefore:

- this package uses **August 21** as the controlling base-RFP question deadline;
- pre-bid attendance/eligibility remains an **UNKNOWN partner/procurement gate** until the actual current TechBid/addenda record or the eligible prime proves it;
- no proposal-readiness claim may depend on an unverified third-party summary.
