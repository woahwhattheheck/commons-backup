## 3. Service A — AI Industry Accelerator

### Common core

Each pathway should teach a repeatable workflow rather than tool hype:

1. define the work objective and authoritative source;
2. classify information that may/may not be entered into an AI tool;
3. write a bounded prompt with role, inputs, output schema, and prohibited actions;
4. inspect generated output against authoritative evidence;
5. record corrections/uncertainty and require human decision ownership;
6. save a reusable checklist or template that does not embed restricted data.

### Five pathway sections

- tools/platforms and licensing status `[OPEN]`;

- practical exercise;
