## Dispositions

Every family ends in one durable state:

- `SOURCE_REAL_CANDIDATE`: source mechanism is real; policy value is still gated.
- `CORRECTED_DESCENDANT`: useful mechanism survives but the literal proposal is replaced by a narrower source-safe form.
- `FALSIFIED`: literal mechanism/policy is disproved and durably fenced.
- `FIELD_BLOCKED`: mechanism/descendant exists but current policy/source/economic form is unsafe, cold, source-unsound, or unproven; a stronger successor gate is required.

Failed literals stay visible. “Make every Gemini idea reach V4” means preserve the best corrected theorem **and** preserve falsifiers/negative receipts so a bad literal cannot be rediscovered as a fresh exploit.
