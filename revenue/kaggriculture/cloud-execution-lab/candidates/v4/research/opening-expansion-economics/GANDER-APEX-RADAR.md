Apex has timed/predator and market behavior that is not conditioned on `is_clone`; those branches remain live. Likewise, one opponent-specific defensive property is not authority to hard-code a universal opening.

The result should therefore be consumed as an opponent-conditioned economics feature, not as a default flip.
