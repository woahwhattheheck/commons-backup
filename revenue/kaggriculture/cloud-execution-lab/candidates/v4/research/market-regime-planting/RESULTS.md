## Constructed activation economics

All four cash-crop prices were explicitly fixed at $1 using the official engine's
configuration. This is not a default-distribution or live-competition result.
Opponent: official starter. Seed: 17. Baseline margin means own cash minus rival
cash; "delta" compares the two full-game trajectories, not just seed spend.

| Seat | Baseline margin | Gate margin | Delta margin | Changed callbacks |

These outcomes reproduce exactly under `python -O`. Different seat outcomes must
not be collapsed into a seat-independent benefit. Neither two constructed seats
nor a no-activation default panel establishes competitive strength.

## Mechanism controls

The helper retains a currently floor-priced carrot when possible future shop
demand can restore its value. Under the ordinary price curves, a constructed
melon inventory of 10,190 has a maximum 30 units of remaining center demand and
no shop demand; even its best final price stays $1, so the direct-revenue ceiling
is $6 against an $80 seed. This latter check is a constructed public snapshot,
not a new completed competitive game.

The suite exercises 24 full-interpreter crop lifecycle continuations (four cash
crops, both seats, three purchase times) and nine pass/pass future-shop
continuations, plus atomic unit/market ordering, live market caps, existing seed
ownership and native seam tests. These continuations are not counted among the
46 full native games. Synthetic inputs are explicitly labeled in source.

## What was and was not integrated

The adapter uses the existing native seed selector on the same instance, before
its finalizers, and preserves unit actions and raw market slots. It defaults OFF.
No production source, config, archive or Kaggle upload was changed. The final
combined V4, true opponent gauntlet, held-out activation and route-reallocation
economics have not been tested. The historical seed-hoard donor and its two
favorable seeds were not recovered; this is independent source.
