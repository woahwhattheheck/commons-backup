# Prime / teaming decision brief

No prime-bid claim is made yet. The controlling package must determine whether Token Junkie Labs can bid directly, must team, or should no-bid.

## Prime path — only if source-proven

A direct response should be considered only if every mandatory bidder gate is source-bound and truthfully evidenced, including any entity/registration, location, insurance, reference, municipal/data-governance experience, staffing, security/privacy, and financial requirements.

## Teaming path

If the technical scope fits but corporate/reference/local-procurement gates do not, prefer a qualified prime or consortium lead while preserving a bounded specialist role. Candidate specialist seams, subject to the actual package, include:

- data-governance evidence and source-custody mechanics;
- data glossary / stewardship workflow implementation;
- responsible-AI control mapping and auditable exception/evidence flows;
- deterministic validation, replay, and change-control tooling;
- technical implementation evidence and acceptance automation.

A partner search should prioritize organizations that can independently evidence the mandatory corporate and public-sector qualifications we cannot. Do not describe a prospect as a partner until there is an actual agreement.

## Questions the controlling package must answer before outreach

- Are consortiums/subcontractors permitted, and how are their qualifications scored?
- Must the prime be Canadian/Albertan, registered locally, or carry specified insurance?
- Are municipal/public-sector references mandatory?
- Is named key-person experience transferable from a subcontractor?
- Are there restrictions on AI-assisted delivery, offshore processing, data residency, or subcontracting?
- Is there a mandatory negotiation/interview/presentation stage?
- What bidder questions may be asked, by what official channel, and by what deadline?

Until those answers are source-bound, status stays HOLD.
