# Draft proposal architecture — non-controlling

This is a **working architecture derived only from the public scope summary**. It is not a representation of the City's required response format. Replace or discard it when the controlling solicitation package is recovered.

## 1. Authority and discovery plan

Establish the agreed source universe, stakeholder map, system/data-domain inventory, decision log, and evidence method. Separate factual discovery from policy decisions that remain with City leadership.

## 2. Data ownership and stewardship operating model

Map data domains to accountable owners and operating stewards; define escalation, decision rights, lifecycle responsibilities, and cross-domain conflict handling. Produce an implementable RACI only after stakeholder validation.

## 3. Business definition and glossary discipline

Define a governed mechanism for canonical business terms, definition owners, source lineage, change proposals, versioning, approval, publication, and conflict/supersession.

## 4. Responsible data and AI use controls

Translate City-approved policy constraints into risk-tiered data/AI use controls, provenance expectations, human decision points, sensitive-data handling, vendor/tool intake questions, incident/escalation routes, and auditable exceptions. Do not invent legal compliance claims; applicable legal/records/privacy duties must come from authoritative sources and City counsel/policy owners.

## 5. Implementation and adoption roadmap

Prioritize near-term governance mechanics, pilot domains, ownership gaps, glossary backlog, tooling/process changes, training/adoption, measurement, and future-state capability without assuming a particular platform purchase.

## 6. Handoff and measurable acceptance

Deliver source-bound decisions, open issues, implementation backlog, governance artifacts, knowledge transfer, and an owner-reviewed roadmap. Acceptance criteria must be rewritten to match the controlling RFP/NRFP once recovered.

## Evidence we may truthfully assemble

Potential internal evidence families include deterministic source/evidence custody, audit/replay controls, data-lineage validation, AI-agent governance, and structured qualification tooling already present in Commons. These are **capability artifacts, not municipal references or buyer acceptance evidence**. Any proposal must distinguish them from required client references, certifications, insurance, or prior-contract proof.
