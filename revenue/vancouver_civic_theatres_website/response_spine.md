# PS20261832-ACCS-RFP — internal response spine

**Status: INTERNAL DRAFT / NOT A SUBMISSION.**

This outline deliberately stops at the boundary of facts exposed by the City of Vancouver public Supplier Portal. It is a work surface for completing the buyer's real annexes after authorized access—not a substitute for those annexes.

## 1. Executive approach

Propose a staged replacement of the Vancouver Civic Theatres website with measurable acceptance at discovery, implementation, launch and operations boundaries. Exact architecture, CMS, migration volume, integration list, delivery schedule and service levels remain subject to the buyer's unrecovered Scope of Work, technical/functional annexes and Form of Agreement.

## 2. Accessibility

The public event requires confirmation of **WCAG Level AA**. The eventual response should bind this to concrete evidence: component/page audit coverage, automated checks, keyboard-only flows, focus/error behavior, contrast, semantic structure, assistive-technology sampling, remediation workflow and pre-launch acceptance. Exact City criteria remain controlled by Annexes 2/3.

## 3. Delivery lifecycle

Candidate internal workstreams, pending buyer-document confirmation:

- discovery and requirement traceability;
- information architecture/content inventory and migration planning;
- experience/design system and responsive implementation;
- CMS and integration implementation;
- automated/manual QA, accessibility and performance evidence;
- UAT, launch rehearsal, cutover and rollback;
- admin training, operational runbook and handoff;
- post-launch maintenance, support and hosting operations.

No page count, CMS choice, named integration, milestone or staffing promise is asserted here.

## 4. Operations and hosting

The buyer's public event explicitly includes post-implementation maintenance, support and hosting. The final proposal should address support intake, severity, response/restoration targets, patching, vulnerability handling, backup/restore, observability, availability, capacity, incident communications, change control and exit/handover—but only at values allowed by the Form of Agreement and technical/functional annexes.

## 5. Commercial response

The public event requests one lump-sum **Total Project Fee for the initial Two-Year Contract** covering Year 1 design/development/implementation and Year 2 maintenance/support for the CMS and hosting environment, exclusive of GST/PST.

Pricing belongs **only** in the City's Annex 4 Financial Proposal. No price is proposed here. Any internal costing model remains `PROPOSED_INTERNAL_NOT_SUBMITTED` until Annex 4 and the Scope of Work are retained and reconciled.
