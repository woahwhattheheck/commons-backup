## Decision

Do not treat this as an open procurement yet.

City of Danville RFP 798, a closed ERP Selection & Advisory Consultant solicitation, explicitly anticipates a separate future ERP implementation project-management solicitation. Its tentative sequence is:

- October 2026: implementation-consultant PM RFP development
- November 2026: PM RFP publication
- December 2026: PM consultant selection and contract
- January 2027: implementation kickoff
- 12–18 months: implementation/go-live
- 3–6 additional months: go-live support

RFP 798 also says its respondents may be eligible for the future procurement at City discretion, and that no future implementation award is guaranteed.

The schedule is not date-certain. The same forecast expected the ERP software RFP in August 2026 and software selection in September. A 2026-09-17 check of the live City bid board found the closed RFP 798 but did not find a separately published ERP software RFP. Treat November as forecast until the City posts the PM package.

## Qualification signals from RFP 798

The predecessor required evidence of:

- public-sector ERP and large multi-department implementation qualifications;
- an actual project team and subconsultants;
- 3–5 public-sector project references;
- phased stakeholder/change/data-conversion/security/training delivery approach;
- municipal vendor forms;
- insurance before contract execution.

Its evaluation used qualifications, experience, approach, and cost-effectiveness. The future PM solicitation may change every one of these terms.

The predecessor insurance requirements include material general-liability, professional-E&O, cyber/technology-E&O, auto, and workers-compensation coverage. Do not assume our current coverage satisfies the future solicitation until the actual package and certificates are checked.
