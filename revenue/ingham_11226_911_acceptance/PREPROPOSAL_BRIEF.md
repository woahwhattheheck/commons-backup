# 112-26 pre-proposal / qualification brief

**Internal only.** This is a question and decision brief for the mandatory September 17, 2026 meeting or an eligible attending partner. It does **not** authorize registration, attendance, email, calls, or submission.

## Objectives

The highest-value purpose of the meeting is not to pitch. It is to resolve procurement and acceptance facts that determine whether an independent verification workshare is useful and allowed.

## Procurement questions

1. Does mandatory pre-proposal attendance attach only to the prime respondent, or must proposed subcontractors/team members also attend/register?
2. May a respondent add a specialist subcontractor after the mandatory meeting if the prime attended?
3. Does the County permit multiple vendors/awards or component/workstream proposals, or must one respondent provide the entire requested solution?
4. Are subcontractors evaluated independently for qualifications/references, and what forms/disclosures are required for them?
5. What is the anticipated contract term and price format?
6. What proposal sections, certifications, insurance requirements, data/security terms, references, and mandatory forms are required?
7. How will technical capability, implementation approach, security, experience, price, and demonstrations be weighted?
8. Will the County publish attendance, Q&A, Addendum 1 and subsequent addenda to registered vendors, and what is the controlling route to obtain them?

## Scope / product-boundary questions

9. Which outcome areas are mandatory versus optional: operations, QA, training/simulation, hiring/recruitment/onboarding, analytics, call handling, evidence, integrations?
10. Is the County seeking one integrated platform, multiple best-of-breed solutions, or either?
11. Which current systems are authoritative for the workflows in scope, and which integrations are mandatory versus desirable?
12. Are vendors expected to write back to operational systems, or may the solution be advisory/read-only for some functions?
13. Which records/outputs must be retained, exportable, auditable, discoverable, or responsive to public-records/FOIA workflows?
14. What are the required human-review/override points for AI/automation-derived outputs?

## Acceptance / evaluation questions

15. What evidence will the County require before accepting AI/automation-assisted QA, training, hiring or operations capabilities?
16. Does the County have a gold-set/adjudicated corpus, current QA rubric, policy/version history, or simulation library that can be made available in an authorized test environment?
17. Should proposed products demonstrate behavior on missing, contradictory, low-quality, stale, duplicate or otherwise difficult inputs?
18. How should unprocessable cases appear in QA-coverage/accuracy metrics—are vendors required to expose denominator loss and abstentions?
19. What accuracy/error metrics matter by use case, and who provides final human adjudication?
20. Are independent acceptance tests, third-party evaluation evidence, or pilot exit criteria desired as contract deliverables?
21. What constitutes a failed pilot versus an accepted pilot with conditions?
22. What model/config/rubric/version changes require revalidation after initial acceptance?

## Security / data questions

23. Which data classes may be processed in demonstrations, evaluation and production (for example recordings, transcripts, CAD metadata, PII, CJI, medical information)?
24. What security framework, identity/RBAC, encryption, hosting/data-residency, retention/deletion and incident-reporting requirements apply?
25. Are synthetic/de-identified fixtures acceptable for pre-production acceptance before restricted-data authorization?
26. What restrictions apply to vendor/model-provider retention, training, telemetry and secondary use of County data?
27. What non-production environment and interface documentation will be available for integration/acceptance testing?

## Reliability / continuity questions

28. What availability, latency, queueing, disaster-recovery and support/service-level objectives apply to each component?
29. How must the product behave when a dependency is unavailable or an input is incomplete—fail closed, queue, degrade, escalate, or another defined mode?
30. What audit evidence is required for retries, duplicate suppression, human overrides, system errors and recovery actions?
31. Which functions, if any, may never become a dependency for live emergency call processing?

## Workforce / value questions

32. Which current manual tasks consume the most supervisor/analyst time and which baseline measures are authoritative?
33. What QA coverage, coaching/training, hiring/onboarding, staff workload, rework/exception, or service-quality outcomes define success?
34. Does the County want measured before/after results as part of implementation, and what measurement window is acceptable?
35. Are workforce/labor stakeholders required in workflow design or pilot acceptance?

## Fast partner qualification

If an eligible vendor/prime approaches TJLabs, obtain these facts before offering a priced workshare:

- proof it attended/satisfied the mandatory meeting gate;
- active pursuit status and intended proposal role;
- exact 112-26 packet/Addendum 1 copy or authorized access;
- whether specialist subcontractors are allowed and how disclosed;
- selected product/workflow(s) to evaluate;
- test environment/data boundary;
- security/privacy owner;
- prime owner for qualifications, certifications, insurance, submission and implementation;
- desired evaluation deliverables and acceptance authority;
- schedule/milestones;
- authorized pricing/subcontract contact.

No partner relationship is represented until the partner explicitly confirms it.
