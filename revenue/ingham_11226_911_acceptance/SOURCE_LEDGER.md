## C. Public County operational context

These are historical/current-environment facts, not requirements of 112-26.

### CAD

Ingham County Board materials show continued support for a **CentralSquare Technologies** CAD environment (originally TriTech). A 2025 renewal resolution listed a one-year support amount of $186,868.15.

### QA / evidence / coaching

County committee minutes from May 2025 discuss **GovWorx CommsCoach AI** for 9-1-1 QA/training. The 9-1-1 Director described the existing QA burden as time intensive, said the then-national evaluation practice was about one percent of calls, and described a goal of evaluating much broader call volume while freeing supervisors for floor/coaching work. This package treats that as evidence of buyer pain and workflow priorities, not as a claim about current vendor status under 112-26.

NiCE Public Safety public customer material also identifies Ingham County 9-1-1 leadership discussing automated evidence production/QA and supervisor time. Again, this is historical/current-environment context, not a solicitation requirement.

### Dispatch protocols / simulation / policy and training

County materials show:

- Priority Dispatch / **ProQA** medical software and call-simulation activity;
- **PowerDMS** policy management/training platform activity;
- a 2026 resolution authorizing contract negotiations with **INdigital** for 9-1-1 telephony; and
- a 2025 professional-services agreement with **getResQ911** to address staffing pressure.

These names establish a heterogeneous public-safety environment and potential integration/evaluation boundaries. They do not establish that any named vendor is responding to 112-26, that its current contract remains active, or that TJLabs has access to the systems.
