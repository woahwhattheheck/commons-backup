# Knowledge-readiness interview and preparation worksheet

Use this instrument for a specific proposed AI-assisted task, not for a blanket
organization score. All worked examples below are fictional. Leave real University
answers unfilled until the relevant evidence and context have been collected.

## Task header — populate during discovery

Task/question ID: ______  Assessment group: ESS / RIS / IAM

Task and intended user outcome: ______  Expected audience: ______

Source collection boundary and capture date: ______  Analyst: ______

Why this task is a useful sample; omitted cases: ______

Success criteria for a useful answer, including unacceptable omissions: ______

Available time/effort baseline and how it was measured, or UNKNOWN: ______

Record existing access and data-use context without requesting credentials or
changing permissions. Keep actual evidence in the engagement's appropriate
storage, not in this public source-code repository.

## Seven dimensions

| Dimension | Interview prompt | Evidence to request | Record distinction | Concrete preparation option |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage | What must an answer include to let someone complete this task? Where do exceptions live? | Representative questions, required facts, exact supporting clauses and known omitted collections | No source in the supplied collection is not proof no source exists anywhere | Locate missing procedures or record a question for the responsible group; rerun the same task |
| Freshness | Who reviews this guidance, how often, and after which changes? | Revision/capture/review dates, declared review interval, change notice, supersession record | Document age, review age and correctness are different | Have the source steward reconcile an identified stale clause; preserve old revision and disposition |
| Provenance | Can an analyst recover the exact supporting text and its origin? | Source locator, revision, content digest, line/section locator, applicable context | Matching bytes do not prove author authenticity or authoritative status | Add exact locators and capture records; confirm source interpretation with the steward |
| Structure | Are terms, units, scope, prerequisites and exceptions explicit? | Examples of ambiguous terms, tables, definitions and linked decision records | Matching exact assertion keys requires analyst interpretation | Add a maintained vocabulary bridge or a structured extraction reviewed against the source |
| Retrieval quality | Can intended users find the relevant source with the words they actually use? | Realistic questions, retained returned IDs, relevance judgments and known missing matches | Retrieval recall is not answer correctness; unknown gold labels are not zero | Label a task set, inspect misses/distractors, improve terminology/indexing and repeat the comparison |
| Ownership | Who can resolve disagreement or maintain this record? | Named organizational role, handoff/escalation record and recent maintenance example | A named owner is not proof maintenance occurred | Agree a maintenance role and record a real correction; avoid scoring an individual employee |
| Existing access semantics | For which audience and purpose is this source already available? What limits are recorded? | Metadata describing existing readership, purpose and known exceptions; no credential values | Known context is not a provider permission check or authority to disclose | Resolve missing context through the engagement's existing contacts; do not broaden access from this worksheet |

For each dimension record: observed artifact and exact locator; interview statement
and speaker role; unknown/missing evidence; contradictory evidence; interpretation
and alternatives; proposed action; proposed responsible role; effort range and
assumption; dependency; evidence that would demonstrate completion.

## Worked preparation backlog — fictional, not institutional recommendations

| Task | Demonstrated friction | Proposed work and role | Effort assumption | Dependency | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ESS retry guidance | Active documents declare three vs five attempts | Documentation steward checks applicable service behavior and records a supersession decision | 1–3 staff hours, illustrative | Appropriate service owner and source records available | Retained old source, explicit dated disposition and newly reviewed active guidance; conflict gone for a justified reason |
| RIS budget handoff | Useful content has unknown source/owner/review/readership metadata | Research-service documentation role supplies those records | 0.5–2 staff hours, illustrative | Source origin and actual owner are discoverable | Complete source metadata and exact supporting quotes; unknowns removed only where evidenced |
| RIS grant-import vocabulary | "Funder records replay cap" returns no source | Documentation/search maintainer adds a reviewed terminology bridge | 1–2 staff hours, illustrative | Practitioners confirm the terms mean the same thing in the intended scope | Same question retrieves the documented source; other retained questions checked for regressions |
| IAM business verification | Contact instructions lack a business-verification step | Operations/documentation roles write the missing step with the service owner | 2–4 staff hours, illustrative | Business function and acceptance behavior identified | Reviewed written procedure covers the required fact; a later exercise would be separate execution evidence |

These effort ranges are planning examples, not quoted prices, staffing commitments
or measured University effort. The executable backlog does not invent effort;
this worksheet supplies explicit assumptions for discussion.

## Answer-support rehearsal

| Question | Responsible before response | Responsible after response | Remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What is the registration retry limit? | Two active records disagree; present both exact clauses and ask the steward to reconcile scope | Cite the three-attempt active record and retain the explicit old-record supersession | Neither the script nor the written record proves live-system behavior |
| What budget validation and handoff are required? | Quote the available guidance while flagging missing provenance and ownership context | Cite the same clauses plus the supplied review/source/ownership records | Supplied metadata must be substantiated in a real engagement |
| What is the funder-records replay cap? | No retrieved source supports an answer to this wording | The reviewed vocabulary bridge leads to the two-attempt grant-import record | The bridge is a fictional semantic judgment, not automatically inferred truth |
| How is sign-in recovery verified? | Give the supported contact role, explicitly state that business verification is absent | Cite the newly written business-verification procedure | A procedure is not a completed recovery exercise |

These are manually authored response patterns tied to the fixture, not measured
language-model outputs. To measure answer usefulness later, retain actual outputs,
blind review where practical, explicit correctness/completeness criteria, time to
repair, and the evidence set used. Keep that result separate from retrieval metrics.

## Operator handoff to the assessment workflow

Run the CLI against a retained snapshot. Inspect `report.md` and open the referenced
source lines in `documents/`. Confirm the assertion's meaning; exact quote matching
alone does not establish interpretation. Inspect the full conflict register, not
just the top-ranked search result. Review relevance labels and collection coverage.

For a candidate AI-readiness observation, record the group, query ID, required fact,
source ID/revision/hash/lines, source-condition flags, and analyst interpretation in
the existing assessment register. Preserve unresolved disagreement and distinguish
source gaps from retrieval gaps. The resulting observation must still pass the
engagement's substantive evidence review before it becomes a finding.

Use the preparation rows as proposed work. After an actual correction, create a new
snapshot rather than relabeling the previous result. Keep the source disposition
and changes in gold labels visible. Re-run the representative questions and review
both improvements and regressions. A new operator should be able to reproduce
reports from the retained manifest without reconstructing citations by memory.

## Real inputs still required

Actual task priorities and audience; authorized source collection and collection
boundary; organizational roles; content/revision/capture/review records; source
applicability and existing readership context; representative user questions;
reviewed assertion interpretations; relevance judgments; actual answer-quality and
repair-effort measurements; agreed follow-up owners and effort estimates. None is
established by the fictional examples.
