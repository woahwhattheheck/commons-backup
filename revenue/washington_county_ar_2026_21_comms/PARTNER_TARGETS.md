# Candidate-prime / teaming targets - public evidence only

These are **research candidates, not partners and not permission to contact them**. Before outreach, check Gmail/Slack/CRM durable receipts and the organization-level outbound lease so only one swarm node contacts an organization.

## 1. Polimorphic - strongest public fit found

Public material describes one local-government platform spanning Voice AI, AI search/chat, CRM/workflows, SMS, email, multilingual resident service, and government integrations. Its own site says it is trusted by hundreds of agencies; current public examples include Polk County, NC; Newport, RI; Tooele County, UT; Augusta, GA; Avondale, AZ; Baldwin County, AL; and a Hernando County chatbot used to find property-tax bills and property records.

Why investigate first:
- strong match to the one-platform government-service requirement;
- public government client footprint plausibly able to satisfy the five-project gate;
- public cross-channel / CRM / integration claims;
- existing public-sector procurement channel through Carahsoft.

Must verify before treating as bid-capable:
- native email behavior exactly satisfies Section 11.4;
- a **production CAMA/property-assessment/tax/permitting system-of-record integration** with a reference, not just website search;
- current WCAG 2.1 AA VPAT/equivalent for the proposed public chatbot;
- ten-business-day two-channel implementation commitment;
- 50,000-voice-interaction economics and all requested price rows;
- willingness to prime BID 2026-21 and use TJLabs as a paid technical validation subcontractor.

Public starting points: `https://www.polimorphic.com/`, `https://www.polimorphic.com/pricing`, and Carahsoft's Polimorphic government page.

## 2. BetaQuick - exact assessor/CAMA marketing fit, evidence depth not yet proven

Public material specifically markets AI voice agents for county assessor offices and names Tyler Eagle, Vision Government Solutions, Patriot Properties, Aumentum, BS&A, and other CAMA/exemption integrations; it also states SAM.gov active and multilingual/audit-log capabilities.

Why investigate:
- unusually exact domain language for assessor surge calls and CAMA integration;
- may have the integration evidence that the bid heavily weights.

Why it is secondary until verified:
- current public search did not surface the five comparable government project references, a one-platform native email/live-chat/case-management footprint, or a VPAT.

## 3. Polimorphic + specialist CAMA integrator

If Polimorphic can satisfy the prime platform and five-project experience gates but lacks a qualifying CAMA production reference, a disclosed CAMA specialist could potentially close that seam. Section 11.5.2 allows partner/subcontractor participation but keeps the prime fully accountable, so the responsibility split must be explicit and the prime must be comfortable owning integration performance and data accuracy.

## Proposed TJLabs paid workshare

A bounded subcontract is more credible than claiming to supply the whole platform:
1. translate Sections 11.1-11.6 into executable acceptance cases;
2. create synthetic and Agency-approved historical inquiry replay suites;
3. verify cross-channel case continuity and knowledge-version consistency;
4. test identity-gated account-specific disclosure;
5. test read-only CAMA adapter behavior, stale/missing/contradictory record handling, and legacy import paths;
6. emit deterministic test receipts and regression evidence for staff sign-off;
7. support the ten-business-day review milestone without touching production data until County IT approval exists.

Commercial intent: **paid subcontract / fixed deliverable**, not indefinite free consulting. Price and terms require explicit commercial approval.
