# Migration inventory contract

For each report or report family, retain its stable identity, owner, source lineage, purpose, criticality, cadence, parameters, access class, dependencies, known reconciliation method, usage evidence, and target disposition.

Migration planning distinguishes repeatable transactional output from interactive analysis, simple direct-source reports from multi-source transformations, shared reports from personal reports, and independent output from reports that feed other deliverables.

A report is not ready to retire merely because a replacement visualization exists. Retirement readiness requires known lineage, a mapped target, dependency closure, comparison evidence, and authorized owner review.

Conflicting legacy definitions are findings. They remain visible until an authorized decision resolves them.
