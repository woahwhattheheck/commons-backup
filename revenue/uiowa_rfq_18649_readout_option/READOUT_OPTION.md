# Optional final-readout support package

**University of Iowa RFQ 18649 | Proposed TJLabs subcontract option | $4,000 fixed**

**Status: PROPOSED / NOT ACCEPTED.** This optional service supplements the proposed $24,000 base technical workshare. It begins only under separate written authorization defining the selected support, timing, participants, and required inputs. No session, individual attendance, travel, or University-facing commitment is created by this description. [E1 §4.4]

## Proposal description

TJLabs proposes to turn the completed technical assessment package into clear, traceable presentation materials and provide evidence support for up to two final-readout sessions. The $4,000 option includes one shared core presentation, supporting appendix, speaker notes, a source lookup map, one consolidated preparation-stage revision round, and remote support for one or two sessions. Materials carry forward the report's strengths, findings, uncertainty, and phased recommendations without adding unsupported conclusions. [S1 p2 WP6; E1 §§4.4, 8-9; D1]

The proposed delivery baseline is a 12-18-slide core presentation with an 8-12-slide appendix and sessions of up to 60 minutes each, including discussion. The same core material serves both sessions; two hours of audience-specific tailoring for a second session are included. These quantities and the remote format are proposed service assumptions for agreement, not University-specified requirements. [D1]

The prospective prime retains presentation strategy, final wording, professional judgment, the University relationship, and all audience, scheduling, and attendance commitments. The RFQ treats follow-on readout/discussion sessions as separately priced options outside the base engagement, potentially selected after delivery of the final written report. [R1; S1 p2 WP6; E1 §9]

## What changes from the base workshare

The base workshare produces the evidence register, technical matrix, technical findings and roadmap inputs, limitations/change records, and consistency/currentness material. The option repackages that work for discussion, adds speaker support and rapid evidence navigation, and provides the selected session support. Base artifact defects remain the responsibility of the existing base conformance/cure process; they are not relabeled as optional presentation work. [E1 §§4.1-4.4, 5, 7]

A second session adds audience tailoring and attendance effort. It does not silently add a new stakeholder assessment, a new evidence universe, a replacement final report, an independently researched second deck, or additional implementation work. [E1 §7; D1]

## Inputs, revision handling, and delivery sequence

**Inputs to agree:** the separately authorized option; the technical package/version to present; its supported findings, unresolved matters, limitations, and evidence references; intended audiences and purpose; prime direction on the narrative; the authorized storage and presentation environment; and the consolidated reviewer/comment route. Missing inputs affect the work dependent on them and stay visible in the plan. Artifact acceptance does not imply agreement with every supported conclusion. [S1 p2 WP6; E1 §§3, 4.4, 5, 6, 8-9]

The proposed sequence is to assemble a shared deck/appendix/notes/map, receive one consolidated preparation-stage comment set, revise it, support the first selected session, apply the included tailoring if a second session is selected, and hand over the resulting coherent package. This is a relative workflow, not a date promise or an additional contractual approval gate. [D1]

The four-hour revision allowance covers ordinary presentation changes within the agreed scope, such as sequencing, emphasis, slide wording, or a different appendix pointer. If requested preference changes exceed that planning allowance, identify the requested additions and effort effect for agreement before undertaking the additional work. Do not assume extra work is approved or incurred. [D1]

Genuine factual mistakes, broken references, or failures in TJLabs-authored materials to meet the agreed artifact description are corrected; the four-hour allowance is not a reason to leave such defects unresolved or automatically charge for their correction. A supported finding disputed on professional-judgment grounds is recorded for prime resolution. Newly supplied evidence is preserved and its effect assessed; material new analysis or a changed deliverable is distinguished from correcting an existing defect. [E1 §§5, 7, 10]

Final materials identify the underlying technical-package version and retain source links, uncertainty, and a change record. This makes the handoff reviewable. It does not assert University acceptance of any finding or add a payment condition. [S1 p2 WP6; E1 §§5, 8, 10]

## Onsite delivery and commercial boundaries

The remote baseline prices production and selected attendance support. The RFQ anticipates onsite readout discussions when reasonable; the prime must reconcile the desired delivery format with its University obligations. This option does not claim that remote subcontractor support alone satisfies every onsite expectation. [R2; D1]

If onsite TJLabs involvement is requested, separately identify attendance effort, travel time, travel expenses, and the treatment of each; avoid counting the same session hours once as remote attendance and again as onsite work. Agree scope, cost treatment, and prior written authorization before committing travel or expenses. The existing source does not establish travel quantities, prices, or anyone's availability. [S1 p3 §5; E1 §§7, 9; D1 accounting method]

The $24,000 base milestone triggers remain unchanged: 40% on written authorization/kickoff, 40% on draft technical-package delivery, and 20% on final technical-package acceptance. This option description introduces no new invoice or payment trigger. The separate written authorization controls the selected optional service and its commercial terms. [E1 §§2, 4.4, 10]
