## Authorized retention exceptions

For every retained item, list:

- evidence ID;
- written University/prime instruction or other controlling authority reference;
- custody location;
- responsible custodian;
- review/end date if stated by the instruction.

If no retention exceptions exist, state **none**. Do not infer permission to retain from silence.

## Unresolved items

List any inventory/disposition mismatch, missing verification artifact, source-locator uncertainty, legal/records hold, or other blocker. A clean-looking template must never be used to hide an unresolved item.
