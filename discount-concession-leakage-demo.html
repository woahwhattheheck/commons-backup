How to read the states

  • FULL_PRICE: billed unit price equals the supplied reference price.
  • AUTHORIZED_DISCOUNT: a supplied authority matches the observed per-unit reduction.
  • UNAUTHORIZED_DISCOUNT_REVIEW: a discount exists and this simplified line has no active authority.
  • DISCOUNT_VARIANCE_REVIEW: an authority exists but its reduction differs from the observed reduction.
  • PRICE_UPLIFT_OBSERVED: billed price is above reference price; visible, but not counted as leakage.
  • HOLD: the local values cannot be evaluated safely.
