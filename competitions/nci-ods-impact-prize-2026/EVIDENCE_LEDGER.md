### Eligibility explained in the recording

At 24:22-28:15 the presenter describes individuals, teams and legally established entities; age 18 or older at submission; U.S. citizen/permanent-resident status for an individual or team leader seeking a cash prize; and U.S. incorporation plus primary U.S. business location for a private entity. Non-U.S. members may join an otherwise eligible team but cannot receive a prize share. The presenter also describes federal/HHS employment and competition-administration/family exclusions. There is no career-stage requirement, and the underlying activity need not have NCI/NIH funding. These are attributed webinar statements, not an exhaustive legal checklist or a claim that a particular entrant qualifies.

   `https://gdc.cancer.gov/turn-data-sharing-ideas-impact-enter-nci-ods-impact-prize`  
   The NCI page says the ODS Impact Prize seeks ideas and successful strategies that advance broad, rapid, equitable sharing and reuse of NCI-funded cancer research outputs. It names Track 1 ideas, Track 2 exemplars, and Track 3 NCI Data Jamboree submissions.    `https://datascience.nih.gov/tools-and-analytics/best-practices-for-sharing-research-software-faq`

   `https://schema.datacite.org/`
