NOURISH Signal Notebook

A local-first prototype for recording diet, nutrition and symptom observations and exploring descriptive within-person associations.

Research/self-tracking prototype. It does not diagnose, treat, recommend diets, or establish that an exposure caused an outcome. Data stays in this browser session unless you explicitly export a file.
