## Key technical thesis

The proposal's strongest published evidence is **wet structural integrity**, not finished-cup compliance: Toivonen et al. report an 80/20 CNF/chitosan nanopaper with ~100 MPa wet ultimate strength and visible transparency. Shellac literature supports a complementary moisture-barrier role. The integration hypothesis is falsifiable: if the shellac-coated formed composite cannot stay <=5% haze while passing 24 h liquid and mechanical/thermal gates, the concept should be rejected rather than re-described as compliant.
