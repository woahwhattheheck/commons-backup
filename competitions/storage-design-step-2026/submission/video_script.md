# 90-second public introduction video — script + shot plan

**0–12 s — Grid storage problem / simple system graphic**
“Long-duration storage needs more than low-cost chemistry. It also needs a product architecture that can be
manufactured, sourced, serviced, and scaled without fragile single-source dependencies.”

**12–30 s — Prior-art boundary / chemistry graphic**
“Peer-reviewed researchers have already demonstrated alkaline all-soluble iron flow batteries, including
iron-gluconate and ferri/ferrocyanide systems. FerroFrame does not claim to invent that chemistry.”

**30–58 s — Exploded cassette animation**
“Our proposal moves the innovation to production: a standardized stack cassette with fewer bespoke wetted parts,
a qualification envelope for more than one membrane source, repeatable assembly interfaces, and a factory-dry
integration path that is only adopted if chemical-safety and logistics review support it.”

**58–76 s — Sensitivity dashboard**
“Instead of promising savings from a sketch, we use a transparent model. Supplier quotes, labor minutes, tooling,
and source concentration are compared against a baseline. If the design cannot reduce conversion cost and
single-source exposure on real evidence, it does not pass.”

**76–90 s — Phase 2 validation ladder**
“Phase 2 would retire risk in order: materials and EHS, membrane compatibility, single-cell performance, stack
repeatability, then quote-backed manufacturability. FerroFrame is a design-to-production hypothesis built to be
measured—and rejected or improved by evidence.”

## Production notes

- Human presenter or owner-approved narration; do not imply a built prototype.
- Use diagrams generated from the actual submission design; no stock footage implying FerroFrame hardware exists.
- Keep third-party lab metrics visually labeled “published literature”.
- Final video must be <=90 seconds and reviewed against current official rules before upload.
