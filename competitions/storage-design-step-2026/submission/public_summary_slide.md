## FERROFRAME
### Supplier-flexible all-iron flow storage designed for manufacturability

**Problem**
Long-duration flow batteries can use abundant active materials, but commercialization can still inherit bespoke
stack hardware, membrane dependence, manual assembly, and water-heavy logistics.

**Design**
A standardized, serviceable stack cassette for alkaline all-soluble iron chemistry:
1. common frame / gasket / flow-field envelope;
2. membrane qualification interface designed around multiple candidate suppliers;
3. reduced bespoke wetted-part count and assembly operations;
4. factory-dry system integration with controlled site electrolyte fill *only if EHS/logistics qualification permits*;
5. quote-backed cost and single-source exposure gates before scale claims.

**Evidence anchor**
Published iron-gluconate / ferri-ferrocyanide research reports long-duration 12–20 h cycling and ~83% energy
efficiency in laboratory systems. Those are third-party results, **not FerroFrame test data**.

**Phase 1 → Phase 2 question**
Can production architecture lower conversion cost and supplier concentration **without sacrificing electrochemical
qualification, safety, serviceability, or lifetime?**
