The stop-path thesis

A budget alert is advice to a person who may be asleep. A trace is a recording of the fire, not an extinguisher. A runaway ends when the agent reaches a boundary it cannot route around: a hard stop path that halts the run, a rollback path that returns state to a known point, and a durable receipt that records what ran, what stopped it, and what it cost. Those are buildable artifacts, and the evidence that they work is a running system — not a slide about one. That is what one business day buys: your failure sentence, reproduced, with the stop path visible and the receipt in hand.
