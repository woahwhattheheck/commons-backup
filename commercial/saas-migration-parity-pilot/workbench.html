01 / Choose the intake contract

Format and reusable plan

General CSV/JSON retains original field names. Reusable CSV plans retain the batch adapter's explicit aliases.

Recognizes saas-migration-csv-intake/v1 and saas-migration-workbench-plan/v1; other formats are rejected.

Plans save explicit mappings and metadata, never the source files. Formats are not silently translated.

Importing a plan selects its adapter and reinspects already selected files. Reconfirm its snapshot facts. Selecting a different file clears that side's capture time and completeness; mappings never imply source approval.
