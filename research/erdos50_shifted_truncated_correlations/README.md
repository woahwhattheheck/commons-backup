# Two-point finite-prime totient products

This is a distinct consumer of the seven-prime saved atoms in [Commons #31398](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/commons/pull/31398). For P={2,3,5,7,11,13,17}, it retains the complete joint distribution of R_P(n) and R_P(n+h) for all 128 lag-divisibility patterns.

The certificate contains 62,500 support pairs: 62,372 new joint masses and 128 copied diagonal masses. Every profile has exact total mass 510510. The reader supplies exact rational rectangle/support conditions, product moments, covariance, marginals and ordinary/weighted support-pair navigation. Weighted mass offsets are not numerical residue ranks.

All 308 actual reader responses, 72 matching inverse pairs, two lag aliases, 1,458 exported lag-one atoms and final caches are saved. Read [SHIFTED_CORRELATIONS_API.md](SHIFTED_CORRELATIONS_API.md) for definitions, proof, source/input boundary, ordering and operation counts. [public_checkpoints.json](public_checkpoints.json) records the native-acknowledged recovery chain.

The old sieve, ratios, atom weights and density-tail bounds remain saved premises. This truncated periodic model does not settle Erdős50's derivative question or establish a full-totient joint density or improved tail bound.
