# Finite cycle and edge partitions

This saved index classifies every partition of every edge subset of the labelled K3,5 into simple cycles and singleton edges. The full graph has 406 decompositions; its minimum is seven pieces, attained by 180 decompositions. All 180 minimum decompositions and 37 complete reader responses are retained.

- [API, conventions, exhaustion argument and source scope](CYCLE_EDGE_PARTITIONS_API.md)
- [Constructor and saved reader](cycle_edge_partitions.cjs)
- [Complete 32,768-state index](k3_5_edge_partition_index.json)
- [Saved reader outputs and traces](saved_reader_queries.json)

The finite edge-partition result does not settle the general Erdős–Gallai linear decomposition conjecture. Shared vertices are allowed; shared edges are not. Cycle rotations, reversal and piece permutations do not create extra objects.
