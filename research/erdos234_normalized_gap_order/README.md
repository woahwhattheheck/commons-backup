# Exact normalized prime-gap order

This finite index uses g(n)/ln(n), with n the zero-based prime index. It covers indices 2 through 1898 from an identified 1,900-prime list.

- [API, exact comparisons and scope](NORMALIZED_GAP_ORDER_API.md)
- [Constructor and saved reader](normalized_gap_order.cjs)
- [Complete order certificates](prime_indices2_1898_index.json)
- [All 36 reader outputs](saved_reader_queries.json)

Integer powers establish the complete order and nine ties without approximate logarithms. The 1,897 records have 1,888 distinct values. The reader supplies exact counts at thresholds a/(d ln b), six inverse-rank checks and rational enclosures for four selected records, with fresh arithmetic reported explicitly.

The old prime sieve and least-factor data are not replayed. These finite empirical counts do not establish the existence or continuity of the limiting density in Erdős 234.
