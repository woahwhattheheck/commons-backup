# Reciprocal subsets of an identified finite prime set

This exact CommonJS index navigates reciprocal sums over the 24 supplied primes through 89. Two saved 4,096-row half tables represent 16,777,216 distinct subsets.

Exactly 10,262,454 sums are below 1, none equals 1, and 6,514,762 are above 1. The saved reader locates the exact nearest sum on either side, supports required/excluded primes, and retains seven inverse rank checks.

The literal prime list comes from the identified #31434 guide. Old factorial valuations and unitary-product computations were not replayed. This finite navigation universe does not resolve Erdős 313's infinitude question or classify arbitrary prime sets. The guide credits the primary definition and records all query work.
