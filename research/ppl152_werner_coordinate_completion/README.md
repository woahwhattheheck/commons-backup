# Werner coordinate completions

An exact new optimizer and saved finite index consuming the authenticated positive form from [Commons #31452](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/commons/pull/31452).

Eight selected coordinates give 256 constraint profiles. The first actual reader solves all 256 subsets for one consistent complex fixture, saves 1,024 positive cover-energy increments, and retains 316 responses with 21 matching inverse ranks. Two additional exact target optimizers are included.

Read [the API guide](COORDINATE_COMPLETION_API.md) for the continuous minimization argument, coordinate order, normalization and custody. The objective is the unnormalized form; evaluated unit-vector ratios are not minimized ratios. No general NPT-bound-entanglement or all-copy conclusion follows.

The old form construction, LDL elimination, ten earlier evaluations and external proofs were not replayed. Complete source, input, inverse certificate, reader plans, full response/cache parts and public checkpoints are retained.
