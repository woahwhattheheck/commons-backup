# Complete six-vertex K4-free triple families

The exact labelled family has **477,965** hypergraphs. Its maximum is **14 edges**, attained by **30** members, all exported in the saved-reader output.

- [Definitions, recurrence, sources and API](K4_FREE_FAMILIES_API.md)
- [CommonJS module](k4_free_families.cjs)
- [Complete decision diagram and cardinality certificate](six_vertex_family_certificate.json)
- [All 22 queries and four conditional tables](saved_reader_queries.json)

The 1,063 decision nodes and 7,599 coefficient cells support exact membership, count/rank/select and conditional completions without a census of all 2^20 edge sets. Required edges 0,1,2 and forbidden edges 3,4,5 leave 9,158 completions with a unique maximum.

The 30 maximum members coincide with the established balanced cyclic construction: 15 partitions into pairs, each with two orientations. This finite classification does not resolve the general Turán problem or claim a new construction or record. Prior source attribution and the unavailable Formal500/OEIS routes are explicit.
