# Quadrature energy and the constant-2 reverse-Markov bound

[QUADRATURE_ENERGY.md](QUADRATURE_ENERGY.md) gives a complete elementary argument from positive polynomial quadrature: nodal derivative bounds imply an exact L2 energy bound and then |p(t)|≤sqrt(2(t+1))≤2. It also derives the needed Gauss/right-Radau exactness and positivity from orthogonality.

The right-Radau nodal constant is exactly 2. For Gauss, the note proves only 1+tau_n≤C_G(n)≤sqrt(2(1+tau_n)); it does not establish the sharper equality in the dated Hager statement. No numerical instance, matrix computation, accepted interpolation result or published numerical check is replayed.
