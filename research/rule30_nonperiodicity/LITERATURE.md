# Literature fence — Rule 30 Prize Problem 1

Pinned: 2026-09-17.

## Official prize

- Wolfram Rule 30 Prizes: https://rule30prize.org/
- Stephen Wolfram, “Announcing the Rule 30 Prizes” (2019): https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2019/10/announcing-the-rule-30-prizes/

Problem 1 asks whether the center column generated by Rule 30 from a single nonzero cell always remains non-periodic. The prize site currently advertises a separate $10,000 prize for this problem. Sponsor review/acceptance is distinct from a mathematical artifact in this repository.

## Period-one frontier — prior art, not ours

David L. Condrey, **Finite Configurations Cannot Generate a Constant Trace in Rule 30**, arXiv:2609.09431v1, submitted 2026-09-08:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09431
- https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09431

The paper classifies the two constant center-trace fibers, proves that no column of a nonzero finite Rule 30 orbit is eventually constant, gives sharp finite-support constant-prefix horizons, and explicitly says that **eventual period two is the next unresolved case**. This carrier claims no novelty for those results.

## Earlier structure

- Erica Jen, “Global properties of cellular automata,” *Journal of Statistical Physics* 43 (1986), 219–242. DOI 10.1007/BF01010579.
- Erica Jen, “Aperiodicity in one-dimensional cellular automata,” *Physica D* 45 (1990), 3–18. DOI 10.1016/0167-2789(90)90169-P.
- Johan Kopra, “Rapid left expansivity, a commonality between Wolfram's Rule 30 and powers of p/q,” *Theoretical Computer Science* 946 (2023), 113668. DOI 10.1016/j.tcs.2022.12.018.
- Eric S. Rowland, “Local nested structure in Rule 30,” *Complex Systems* 16(3) (2006), 239–258. DOI 10.25088/ComplexSystems.16.3.239.

The facts used here are deliberately modest: Rule 30 is left permutive; Jen's results imply a finite Rule 30 orbit has at most one eventually periodic column; width-two aperiodicity does not decide whether one exceptional column can be periodic. None of those results is upgraded into a full center-column proof here.

## Claim discipline

This repository's new result is a bounded exact fiber reduction/enumeration and the elementary period-two reduction written in `README.md`. It is **not**:

- a proof of Rule 30 Prize Problem 1;
- a proof excluding all eventual period-two traces;
- a sponsor submission;
- an award or payment claim.
