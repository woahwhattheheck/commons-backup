## Material agreement flags — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED

This is not legal advice. It is a source-derived checklist so registration is not mistaken for a routine form submission.

1. **Registration includes affirmative agreement acceptance.** The guidelines direct entrants to accept the Licensing Agreement as part of registration; the agreement states the relevant checkbox is an electronic signature. Do not click it as an automation step.
2. **Competition-data license.** Section 2.4 describes a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, broad license over Competition Data generated/submitted during participation, surviving exit/termination.
3. **Submission/promotional license.** The pitch template and agreement grant broad perpetual promotional rights over submitted pitch/video/materials.
4. **Original-code rights are not the whole story.** The agreement says entrant original code remains owned by the entrant while granting Forward Edge-AI broad rights over submissions/data under other sections. Read the exact current text before relying on a summary.
5. **$1M annual revenue condition after public release.** Section 9 states the licensed application must reach at least $1,000,000 annual Gross Revenue during each measurement period after public app release; shortfall plus failed cure can trigger license-reversion consequences described there.
6. **Regulatory/export obligations.** The agreement assigns applicable regulatory costs/approvals to the participant and includes export/sanctions obligations. SpectraPass is intentionally pitched as a non-medical industrial workflow to avoid making diagnostic/clinical claims, but legal applicability still requires owner review.
7. **Restricted jurisdictions.** Eligibility excludes listed jurisdictions. Entrant/team identity must be checked against the current list before acceptance.
