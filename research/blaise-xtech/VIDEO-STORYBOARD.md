# SpectraPass — Phase I video storyboard

**Status:** recording-ready draft; no video has been recorded or uploaded. Official Phase I requires a short video of no more than three minutes. Final speaker identity and any public/unlisted upload are `OWNER_PASTE_REQUIRED`.

## Target runtime: 2:20–2:35

### 0:00–0:18 — Hook
**Visual:** Two nearly identical containers or material cards labeled only with neutral synthetic IDs such as A17 and A18. Phone mockup beside a Blaise device illustration.

**Voiceover:**  
“On a maintenance floor, the wrong adhesive, lubricant, coating, or polymer can look exactly like the right one. The paperwork may say what it is. SpectraPass is designed to help verify the material itself before it gets used.”

### 0:18–0:45 — The problem
**Visual:** Simple three-step chain: RECEIVED → STORED → USED, with a mismatch icon between stored and used.

**Voiceover:**  
“Industrial teams already manage labels, purchase records, and quality procedures, but point-of-use identity checks can still require specialist equipment or a lab escalation. A mix-up discovered after installation means rework, downtime, and a harder traceability problem.”

### 0:45–1:18 — The Blaise workflow
**Visual:** Four panels: Scan → Compare → MATCH / MISMATCH / INCONCLUSIVE → Audit record.

**Voiceover:**  
“SpectraPass pairs Blaise Raman data with the organization’s own approved-material library. A technician scans the material, the app compares its spectral fingerprint with versioned references, and it returns one of three operational results: match, mismatch, or inconclusive. Every result can carry the lot, device, timestamp, nearest reference, and calibrated confidence into an auditable record.”

### 1:18–1:43 — AI that can abstain
**Visual:** Confidence gauge that deliberately stops below a decision threshold and flips to INCONCLUSIVE.

**Voiceover:**  
“The important AI feature is not just classification—it is knowing when not to classify. SpectraPass is built around uncertainty calibration, unknown-material tests, drift checks, and explicit abstention. If the spectrum is outside the validated reference set, the app routes the sample to the organization’s existing verification process instead of inventing certainty.”

### 1:43–2:05 — Feasibility and scale
**Visual:** One facility library expanding to multiple sites while the inference core stays constant.

**Voiceover:**  
“Phase II starts small: a bounded material set, repeatable scan procedures, classical spectral baselines, held-out lots, and then compact neural methods only where they measurably improve the result. The same inference engine can scale across sites while each organization controls its own approved reference library and procedures.”

### 2:05–2:28 — Commercial path + close
**Visual:** App-store icons, enterprise site cards, then the SpectraPass name.

**Voiceover:**  
“The commercial model is a B2B subscription for site libraries, audit history, team administration, and enterprise integrations. A bottom-up target of 250 organizations across four sites each at 85 dollars per site per month crosses one million dollars in annual recurring revenue. Select SpectraPass to make material provenance measurable at the point where it matters: before use.”
