What you provide

  • An owner-controlled price policy for the selected customer/service/currency and closed billing period.
  • A complete billed-line snapshot for that same scope, with stable line/source references.
  • Any explicit discount or concession authorities you want evaluated against the billed lines.

Use opaque references where possible. Do not send credentials, card/bank data, secrets, or unnecessary personal information.

What the desk produces

OutputMeaning
FULL_PRICEThe billed unit amount matches the supplied reference price.
AUTHORIZED_DISCOUNTAn active explicit authority matches the observed per-unit reduction.
UNAUTHORIZED_DISCOUNT_REVIEWA positive discount appears with no matching active authority in the supplied evidence.
DISCOUNT_VARIANCE_REVIEWAn active authority exists but its authorized reduction does not match the billed reduction.
PRICE_UPLIFT_OBSERVEDThe billed unit amount is above the supplied reference price; visible, but not treated as leakage.
HOLDThe evidence is incomplete, stale, conflicting, aliased, out of scope, unsafe to compute, or otherwise insufficient for a deterministic classification.

Acceptance boundary

  • Every supplied billed line is accounted for exactly once after exact replay collapse.
  • All monetary calculations use integer minor units with safe-range checks.
  • Scope drift, changed duplicate IDs, source aliases, conflicting authorities, stale/future/incomplete snapshots, and unsafe arithmetic fail closed.
  • The delivered package includes canonical JSON, an owner-readable Markdown summary, a SHA-256 receipt, and an offline semantic verifier.
  • Verification requires the separately retained source root and trusted evaluation time; the packet cannot authenticate itself by resealing changed evidence or a changed evaluation clock.

What this is not

This desk is decision support, not a legal, accounting, tax, audit, collections, or revenue-recognition opinion. It does not contact customers, change invoices, issue credits/refunds, collect money, mutate your billing provider, or assert that any flagged amount is legally owed or recoverable.
